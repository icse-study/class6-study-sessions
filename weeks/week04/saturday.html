Word problems, step by step
The five-step method, then the formulas and worked examples for cost, perimeter, area, speed, percentage, fractions, interest and simple equations.
📐 The five-step method
1. Given — write down what you know · 2. To find — write what is asked · 3. Formula — write it before substituting · 4. Calculate — show the working · 5. Answer with units — ₹, cm, m², km/h. A right number with no unit loses marks.
💰 Unitary method, percentage & interest

Unitary method

Find the value of one, then multiply for many.

1 kg apples = ₹120 → 5 kg = 5 × 120 = ₹600.

Percentage of an amount

x% of N = (x ÷ 100) × N

20% of ₹500 = (20/100) × 500 = ₹100.

Fraction of a quantity

"Of" means multiply.

¾ of 240 pages = (3 × 240) ÷ 4 = 720 ÷ 4 = 180 pages.

Simple interest

SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100

P = ₹2000, R = 5%, T = 2 years → (2000 × 5 × 2) ÷ 100 = 20000 ÷ 100 = ₹200.

🔢 Fractions, decimals & equations

Fraction → decimal, and mixed fractions

Divide the numerator by the denominator. To add mixed fractions, add the whole numbers and the fractions separately.

7/8 = 7 ÷ 8 = 0.875
4½ + 2¼ → wholes 4+2 = 6; halves ½ + ¼ = 2/4 + 1/4 = ¾ → 6¾

Decimal subtraction

Line up the decimal points, fill empty places with zeros, then subtract as usual.

50.00 − 14.25 = 35.75

HCF by prime factorisation, and quantity as a fraction

For HCF, take the common prime factors. To write one quantity as a fraction of another, both must be in the same unit first.

24 = 2³ × 3, 36 = 2² × 3² → common 2² × 3 = 12
450 g of 1 kg = 450/1000 → divide both by 50 → 9/20

Simple equations

Do the opposite operation to both sides to leave x alone.

x + 15 = 35 → subtract 15 from both sides → x = 35 − 15 = 20.

📜 What history is

History and its sources

History is the study of the human past — how people lived, what they did and how societies changed over time.

For ancient periods, when there are few or no readable written records, the main source is archaeological remains — the tools, pottery, bones, coins, seals and ruined buildings dug out of the ground.

Written sources (manuscripts, inscriptions) become the main source only for later periods. Monuments help us understand the art, religion, technology and daily life of the people who built them, which is why they are protected as heritage.

🕉️ The Vedic period

Life and texts

The sacred texts of this age are the Vedas — Rigveda, Samaveda, Yajurveda and Atharvaveda. The Rigveda is the oldest.

In the early Vedic period people were mainly pastoral — cattle rearing was the chief occupation and cattle were the main measure of wealth. In the later Vedic period agriculture became the main occupation as people settled down.

Most ancient civilisations of the world — Indus, Egyptian, Mesopotamian, Chinese — grew on the banks of rivers, because rivers gave water, fertile soil and easy transport.
🗳️ Civics — democracy & local government

Democracy

Democracy is a form of government in which the people rule — they elect their own representatives to govern on their behalf. Abraham Lincoln called it "government of the people, by the people, for the people".

Citizens' primary role is to vote and choose their representatives — and then to hold them accountable, obey the law and take part in public life. In India the minimum voting age is 18 years.

The three organs of government

1. Legislature — makes the laws (Parliament, State Assemblies).
2. Executive — carries out the laws (President, Prime Minister, Council of Ministers).
3. Judiciary — interprets the laws and delivers justice (Supreme Court, High Courts).

💡 Local self-government means people manage their own local affairs — roads, water, sanitation, street lights — through bodies they elect themselves.

💡 Light & electricity

Laws of reflection

1. The angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection (∠i = ∠r).
2. The incident ray, the reflected ray and the normal all lie in the same plane.

A plane mirror forms an image that is virtual, erect, the same size as the object, as far behind the mirror as the object is in front — and laterally inverted (left and right swapped).

Circuits

A closed circuit has no gaps, so current flows continuously and the bulb glows. An open circuit has a break (switch off), so no current flows.

💨 Gases & the air

Key gas facts

Oxygen supports combustion — nothing burns without it (though oxygen itself does not burn). Hydrogen is the lightest gas known.

Air is a mixture. By volume its main component is nitrogen (about 78%), then oxygen (about 21%), then argon, carbon dioxide and water vapour.

💡 Noble gases (helium, neon, argon…) are chemically unreactive because their outermost shell is already completely filled with electrons, so they have no need to gain, lose or share any.

Litmus test

Acid turns blue litmus RED. A base (alkali) turns red litmus blue. A neutral substance changes neither.

Remember: "Acid → Always Red."
