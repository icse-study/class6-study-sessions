Welcome to Cell City 🏙️
Imagine a plant cell as a walled city. Every part of the cell has a job in the city — learn the map and you'll never forget an organelle.

🎲 Fun Corner — which building?
1. The power goes out in Cell City. Which part stopped working?
👀 Show answer
The mitochondria.
2. A truck needs permission to enter.
👀 Show answer
The cell membrane.
3. Cell City in an animal has no outer wall and no solar kitchens. Why?
👀 Show answer
Animal cells have no cell wall or chloroplasts.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🧬 If all the DNA in one of your cells were stretched out, it would be about 2 metres long.
• ⚡ Muscle cells have many more mitochondria than skin cells, because they need lots of energy.
• 🥜 The word "nucleus" comes from the Latin for a nut or kernel — the hard centre of something.
