🌱 After the journey — germination check

A seed lands. Will it grow?

Only if it gets water, air (oxygen) and warmth. Seeds swallowed by birds often germinate better — the gut softens the seed coat.

💡 The coconut's fibrous mesocarp (coir) holds air, so it floats across seas.
