🧭 India mineral map

📝 Exam traps

• Man used metals from the Bronze Age, not the Iron Age.
• Hydroelectricity is renewable.
• Shaft mining is large-scale; alluvial is small-scale.
• Coal = non-metallic energy mineral.
• India IS a major producer of mica.
