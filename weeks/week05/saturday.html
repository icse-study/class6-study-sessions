➗ Maths — How to Attack a Word Problem

Four steps, every time

1. Read it twice. 2. Write down what you must FIND. 3. Decide which operation and in what order. 4. Check the units and whether the answer is sensible.

Signal words: fencing, running around, wire bent → perimeter. Carpeting, levelling, painting → area. Left, remaining → subtract at the end. Cost of many from the cost of a few → unitary method (find ONE first).
🏛️ History — The Indus Valley Civilization

The cities

Two great sites: Harappa (on the river Ravi) and Mohenjodaro (on the Indus). Streets ran in a grid, crossing at right angles, and every house drained into a covered underground drain. Houses were built of standard baked bricks.

They used bronze (copper + tin) for tools, and their pictographic script has never been deciphered.

The Vedic age

The sacred texts are the Vedas — Rigveda, Samaveda, Yajurveda, Atharvaveda. Life centred on cattle rearing and agriculture.

⚡ Physics — Speed & Units

The two conversions you must know

km/h → m/s: multiply by 5/18. So 72 km/h = 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s.
m/s → km/h: multiply by 18/5. So 15 m/s = 15 × 18/5 = 54 km/h.

Uniform motion covers equal distances in equal intervals of time; non-uniform motion does not. A speedometer shows speed, an odometer shows distance travelled. 1 hour = 3600 seconds.
⚗️ Chemistry — Separation in Daily Life

Which method, and why

Evaporation gets salt from sea water. Centrifugation spins cream out of milk. Filtration traps an insoluble solid — what stays on the paper is the residue, what runs through is the filtrate. Oil and water separate by decantation because they do not mix.

Water is unusual: it expands when it freezes, which is why ice floats and why pipes burst in winter. 0 °C = 273.15 K.
