The big lesson

An adaptation is only useful in the habitat it evolved for. Moving an organism to a very different habitat can kill it — which is why destroying habitats is so dangerous for wildlife.

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🦒 A giraffe's long neck helps it reach tall acacia leaves — in a place without tall trees, that advantage disappears.
• 🐧 Penguins in zoos in warm countries live in chilled, air-conditioned enclosures.
• 🌍 Loss of habitat is the biggest threat to wild animals worldwide.
