🌍 Geography — Major Water Bodies

Ocean, sea, river, lake — know the difference

Ocean — the largest salt-water bodies; there are five.
Sea — smaller than an ocean and partly enclosed by land (Mediterranean Sea, Arabian Sea).
River — flowing fresh water that rises in mountains or lakes and ends in a sea or ocean.
Lake — a body of standing water surrounded by land (Lake Superior, Wular, Dal).

💡 A sea is part of an ocean. That is why the Mediterranean is a sea, never an ocean.

Records worth remembering

Largest ocean: Pacific · Smallest ocean: Arctic · Longest river: the Nile · Largest inland water body: the Caspian Sea (a lake, despite its name) · Saltiest: the Dead Sea.

The water cycle — in order

Evaporation (water → vapour) → condensation (vapour → droplets, forming clouds) → precipitation (rain, snow, hail) → runoff (water flows back to rivers and the sea).

Only about 3% of Earth's water is fresh, and most of that is locked in ice. Rivers and lakes hold a tiny fraction.

Tides, and why oceans matter

Tides are the regular rise and fall of sea level, caused mainly by the gravitational pull of the Moon (and partly the Sun). Oceans give us food, salt, transport routes and rain — and they moderate climate, because water heats and cools far more slowly than land.

💡 That slow heating is why coastal Mumbai has a much smaller temperature range than inland Delhi.
✍️ English — Active and Passive Voice

What voice means

Active voice — the subject does the action: The cat chased the mouse.
Passive voice — the subject receives the action: The mouse was chased by the cat.

Same event, different focus. Active spotlights the doer; passive spotlights what was done.

The three steps of the change

1. The object becomes the new subject.
2. Use the right form of be (is / am / are / was / were / will be) to match the tense.
3. Use the past participle (V3) of the main verb, then add by + the old subject.

💡 Sentences with no object (“She sleeps”) cannot be turned into the passive at all.
TenseActivePassive
Simple presentShe writes a letter.A letter is written by her.
Simple pastShe wrote a letter.A letter was written by her.
Simple futureShe will write a letter.A letter will be written by her.
Present continuousShe is writing a letter.A letter is being written by her.

Pronouns change too

I → me · we → us · he → him · she → her · they → them.

I ate the pizza → The pizza was eaten by me — not “by I”.
