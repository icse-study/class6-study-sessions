North America — Location, Boundaries & Countries — think and write
Wednesday's topic, one level deeper. Write each answer in your notebook first (4–6 lines), then open the model answer and compare. Then take the 10-question Geo Quiz.
1. Enumerate the major seas and oceans surrounding North America.
👀 Show model answer
North — Arctic Ocean (with Beaufort Sea, Baffin Bay, Hudson Bay); South — Gulf of Mexico and Caribbean Sea; East — Atlantic Ocean (Labrador Sea); West — Pacific Ocean (Gulf of Alaska, Bering Sea).
2. Describe the location and extent of North America.
👀 Show model answer
Entirely in the Western and Northern Hemispheres; 7°N–83°N and 59°W–180°W; about 9,355,000 sq miles; mostly between the Arctic Circle and Tropic of Cancer; Atlantic to the east, Pacific to the west.
3. List any three countries of North America and their capitals.
👀 Show model answer
Canada — Ottawa; USA — Washington, D.C.; Mexico — Mexico City (also Cuba — Havana).
4. How did North America get its name? Why are the Caribbean islands called the West Indies?
👀 Show model answer
Columbus reached it in 1492 seeking a route to India; he mistook it for India and called it Indies — so the Caribbean islands became the West Indies. Later it was named after Amerigo Vespucci.
5. Differentiate between a strait and an isthmus with examples from North America.
👀 Show model answer
Strait: narrow sea linking two water bodies and separating two lands — Bering Strait (Asia/North America). Isthmus: narrow land with sea on both sides linking two lands — Isthmus of Panama (North/South America).
6. Name the countries of Central America with capitals.
👀 Show model answer
Belize — Belmopan; Guatemala — Guatemala City; Honduras — Tegucigalpa; El Salvador — San Salvador; Nicaragua — Managua; Costa Rica — San José; Panama — Panama City.
7. How did the first humans reach North America, and how did Europeans arrive much later?
👀 Show model answer
First humans walked across a land bridge from Asia to Alaska during the last ice age. Europeans arrived by sea — Columbus in 1492, followed by Vespucci.
8. Why is North America said to lie entirely in the Northern Hemisphere?
👀 Show model answer
Its southern tip (Panama) is at about 7°N, so all of it is north of the Equator.
9. Name five natural wonders or animals that make North America remarkable.
👀 Show model answer
Niagara Falls, Old Faithful Geyser, giant redwood trees, the bison, the grizzly bear (also Grand Canyon, Great Lakes).
10. Name six Caribbean nations with capitals.
👀 Show model answer
Cuba — Havana; Jamaica — Kingston; Haiti — Port-au-Prince; Dominican Republic — Santo Domingo; Bahamas — Nassau; Barbados — Bridgetown.
