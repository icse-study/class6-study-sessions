ಸಮಾನಾರ್ಥಕ & ವಿರುದ್ಧ ಪದಗಳು

Synonyms: ನೀರು = ಜಲ · ಸೂರ್ಯ = ರವಿ = ಭಾನು · ಮನೆ = ಗೃಹ · ಮರ = ವೃಕ್ಷ

Opposites: ಸುಖ ↔ ದುಃಖ (happiness ↔ sorrow) · ದೊಡ್ಡ ↔ ಸಣ್ಣ · ಹಗಲು ↔ ರಾತ್ರಿ · ಸುಲಭ ↔ ಕಠಿಣ

✍️ ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ

ವಚನ, ಲಿಂಗ ಮತ್ತು ನಾಮಪದ

ವಚನ: things take -ಗಳು → ಪುಸ್ತಕ → ಪುಸ್ತಕಗಳು; people take -ರು → ಹುಡುಗ → ಹುಡುಗರು.

ಲಿಂಗ pairs: ಹುಡುಗ ↔ ಹುಡುಗಿ · ಅಣ್ಣ ↔ ಅಕ್ಕ · ರಾಜ ↔ ರಾಣಿ.

ನಾಮಪದ types: ಅಂಕಿತ ನಾಮ = proper noun (ಮೈಸೂರು, ರಾಮ) · ಅನ್ವರ್ಥ ನಾಮ = common noun (ನಗರ, ಹುಡುಗ).

The verb (ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದ) comes last in a Kannada sentence. In "ಮಗು ನಕ್ಕಿತು" (the child laughed), ಮಗು is the noun and ನಕ್ಕಿತು is the verb.

📚 ಸಾಹಿತ್ಯ · Literature

ರಾಷ್ಟ್ರಕವಿ · The national poet of Karnataka

Kuvempu — the pen name of K. V. Puttappa — is Karnataka's best-known ರಾಷ್ಟ್ರಕವಿ. He wrote the state anthem Jaya Bharata Jananiya Tanujate and was the first Kannada writer to receive the Jnanpith Award.

💡 M. Govinda Pai was the first writer to be given the title Rashtrakavi in Karnataka; Kuvempu received it later and is the name most often asked for.

Plateau and archipelago

A plateau is a raised, flat-topped area of land standing above the surrounding land — often called a tableland. India's largest is the Deccan Plateau in the south.

An archipelago is a group or chain of islands. Near India: the Andaman and Nicobar Islands in the Bay of Bengal, and Lakshadweep in the Arabian Sea.

India's land neighbours

Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar and Afghanistan share a land boundary with India.

💡 Sri Lanka and the Maldives are neighbours too, but they are island nations — they share only a maritime (sea) boundary, not a land one.

Why monuments matter

Historical monuments are primary evidence of the past. Their design, materials and carvings tell us about the art, architecture, religion, technology and daily life of the people who built them — things no written record may have recorded. That is why they are protected as national heritage and why damaging them destroys evidence that can never be replaced.

🏆 Sports this week

🎾 Wimbledon 2026 reaches its climax

The historic Wimbledon Championships ended with the finals at SW19, London. Wimbledon is the oldest tennis tournament in the world and the only Grand Slam still played on grass.

The four Grand Slams are the Australian Open (hard court), the French Open / Roland-Garros (clay), Wimbledon (grass) and the US Open (hard court).

⚽ 2026 FIFA World Cup knockout stage

The World Cup moved from the group stage into the knockout rounds and quarter-finals. It is hosted jointly across the USA, Canada and Mexico — the first World Cup shared by three nations.

💡 In a knockout round the losing team is out immediately — there is no second chance, unlike the group stage.
🔬 Science & technology this week

🛰️ A shoebox-sized satellite hunting nuclear signatures

Researchers described a shoebox-sized satellite built to detect hidden nuclear signatures from space. Very small satellites like this are far cheaper and quicker to launch than traditional ones.

💎 Diamond quantum sensors on the ISS

Experiments aboard the International Space Station tested diamond-based sensor cubes working as quantum magnetometers — instruments that measure and map magnetic fields with extreme precision.

A magnetometer measures magnetic fields. The simplest kind you already know is a compass needle.

Adjective or adverb?

A word describing a noun is an adjective. A word describing a verb is an adverb.

"The dog barked loudly." — loudly tells us HOW the dog barked, and barked is a verb → adverb.
