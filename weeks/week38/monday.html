✍️ Writing Practice — answer on paper first (not scored)
1. Compare the generosity shown in “Little Women” with the friendship shown in “Huckleberry Finn”.
👀 Show model answer
The March sisters give away their breakfast to strangers, choosing kindness over their own hunger. Huck risks his reputation and breaks the law to protect Jim, choosing friendship over what society told him was right. In both, characters give up something important for someone else — and are better people for it.
