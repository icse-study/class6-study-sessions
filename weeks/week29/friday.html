🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🎾 Villi, alveoli and capillaries all solve one problem — how to pack a huge surface into a small body.
• 🔁 Your blood makes a full trip round the body in about one minute at rest.
• 🧍 Laid end to end, your capillaries alone would be tens of thousands of kilometres long.
