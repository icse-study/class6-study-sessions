The two differences that carry the marks

A plant cell has a cell wall and chloroplasts; an animal cell has neither. The wall keeps the cell from bursting when water pressure inside rises, and gives the plant its rigid shape.

The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves. The nucleus is the control centre. The vacuole stores food, water and waste. The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

🧬 Biology — The Leaf & The Flower

Parts of a leaf

Lamina (the broad flat blade) · Petiole (the stalk joining it to the stem) · Midrib (the thick central vein) · Veins (the branching network).

Functions: photosynthesis, transpiration and respiration. Reticulate venation is net-like (mango, peepal); parallel venation runs in straight lines (banana, grass).

Parts of a flower — outside in

Calyx (the green sepals, protecting the bud) → Corolla (the coloured petals, attracting pollinators) → Androecium (stamens — anther + filament, the male part) → Gynoecium (pistil — stigma + style + ovary, the female part).

The anther makes pollen. The ovary protects the ovules and later becomes the fruit.
