🧬 Friday · Week 18 · Chapter 7 (part 1)
Health and the diseases you can't catch 🩺
Being healthy is more than "not being ill". This week: what health means, what disease is, and the non-communicable diseases — the ones that do not spread from person to person — especially those caused by a poor diet.

🗺️ Today's route

1️⃣ Health · 2️⃣ Disease & its causes · 3️⃣ Two big categories · 4️⃣ Kinds of non-communicable disease · 5️⃣ Protein-energy deficiency · 6️⃣ Vitamins · 7️⃣ Minerals · 8️⃣ Allergies · 🎲 Fun Corner

1️⃣ Health

The definition

"Health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity."

Signs of health: clear skin · bright eyes · body neither too fat nor too thin · fresh breath · good appetite · sound sleep · regular bladder and bowels · well-coordinated movements
2️⃣ Disease

Dis + ease = not at ease

A disease is a departure from normal health — a disorder of the body's structure or working.

Causes: dirty (unhygienic) surroundings · poor diet · tobacco, alcohol and drugs · germs · body organs not working properly · allergies

8️⃣ Allergies

An over-reaction

An allergy is an unpredictable reaction of the body to a particular substance, an allergen — dust, spores, pollen, certain clothes, medicines or cosmetics. It can affect the skin, breathing or digestion: asthma, eczema, hay fever, diarrhoea, vomiting.

🎯 Fun Trivia
• ⛵ From the late 1700s, British sailors were given lemon and later lime juice to prevent scurvy — which is how they got the nickname "limeys".
• ☀️ Sunlight has no vitamin D in it! UV rays make your skin produce vitamin D.
• 📿 Pellagra often leaves a dark ring of rough skin around the neck, called "Casal's necklace".
• 🍚 Beri-beri spread in Asia when people began eating polished white rice — polishing removes the thiamine-rich outer layer.
