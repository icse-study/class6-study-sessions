🌍 Geography · Ch 4 Agriculture · Part 4
Crops of the World & Cropping Seasons
Food crops, cash crops, fibre, horticulture and beverage crops — where are they grown? Plus India's kharif, rabi and zaid seasons and traditional vs modern farming.

🍚 Food crops

Grown for human consumption: rice, wheat, millets, oats, barley, corn (maize) and pulses. Cereals are grains (rice, wheat, maize) that form the staple diet. Pulses (moong, masoor, arhar) are legumes — rich in protein and they fix nitrogen in the soil.

💡 Legumes = plants with pods (peas, beans, pulses).

📏 Extensive vs intensive cereals

Wheat on the vast semi-arid grasslands is extensive farming; rice in the small fragmented fields of monsoon Asia is intensive farming.

💰 Cash crops

Grown only to sell. They earn money for farmers and foreign exchange for the country, and supply raw materials to industries. In temperate lands even wheat, corn and oats can be cash crops.
• Fibre: jute, cotton, flax, hemp
• Horticultural: flowers (roses, tulips), fruits, vegetables
• Plantation: tea, coffee, rubber, spices, sugar cane, cocoa

🎋 Purpose decides the class

Sugar cane is a cash crop in Australia but a food crop in India. Classification depends on the purpose of cultivation.

🗺️ Distribution of major crops

Tea: Nilgiri Hills (Tamil Nadu), Darjiling (West Bengal), Kandy Plateau (Sri Lanka).
Coffee: Brazilian, Mexican and Colombian Highlands.
Rice: lowlands of China, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Japan.
Wheat: rolling grasslands of USA, Canada, Argentina, Ukraine.

📅 India's cropping seasons

Kharif (summer): sown June–July, harvested Oct–Nov — rice, sugar cane, cotton, jute.
Rabi (winter): sown Oct–Nov, harvested March — wheat, mustard, gram.
Zaid: short summer season between rabi harvest and kharif sowing — seasonal fruits and vegetables for extra income.

⚖️ Traditional vs modern farming

Traditional: more manual labour (more jobs); depends on monsoon so fewer crops a year; natural manure and pest control (less harm); lower yield.
Modern: machines (less labour); irrigation allows dry-season crops; chemical fertilisers and pesticides (pollution); large yield.

🎉 Harvest festivals

Farmers celebrate a good harvest — Lohri in Punjab, Pongal in Tamil Nadu, and (closer to home) Makara Sankranti in Karnataka.

🎯 Must-remember facts
• Food: rice, wheat, millets, oats, barley, maize, pulses · Pulses = legumes = protein + nitrogen
• Fibre: jute, cotton, flax, hemp · Horticulture: flowers, fruit, veg · Plantation: tea, coffee, rubber, spices, sugar cane, cocoa
• Tea: Nilgiris, Darjiling, Kandy · Coffee: Brazil, Mexico, Colombia highlands
• Kharif Jun–Jul → Oct–Nov · Rabi Oct–Nov → Mar · Zaid in between
