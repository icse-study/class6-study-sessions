🔁 Quick recap

Golden statue with sapphire eyes, a ruby sword and a gold heart → Mother tells Daughter the legend of the generous King → the swallow, bound for Egypt, rests and feels the Prince’s tears → ruby to the poor seamstress → sapphire to the writer → sapphire to the match-girl; the Prince is blind → the swallow stays; gold given leaf by leaf → the swallow dies → the Mayor destroys the “worthless” statue → the Angel takes the heart and the swallow to God.

📋 Grammar checklist

Binomial expressions · direct and indirect speech (tense, pronoun, time and place changes) · colon · stress shift · describing a process.

✍️ Writing Practice — answer on paper first (not scored)
1. What kind of friendship developed between the swallow and the Happy Prince?
👀 Show model answer
At first the swallow only wanted to rest before flying on to Egypt, and helped reluctantly. But each good deed made him happier, and when the Prince became blind he refused to leave him. Their friendship became loving, loyal and selfless — the swallow gave up his journey and finally his life for his friend.
