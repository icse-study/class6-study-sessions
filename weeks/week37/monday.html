🔁 Recap

Saviours of the Train — Bobbie, Peter and Phyllis stop the 11:29 after a landslide, waving red scarves.
Gathering Leaves — leaves weigh next to nothing, “but a crop is a crop”.
The Butterfly Mountain’s Shadow — a night climb up Adam’s Peak, butterflies, and the peak’s triangular shadow at dawn.
Across the Border — a day in fairyland, then home to common flowers and brown birds.
