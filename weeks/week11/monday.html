Chapter 12 · Section 12.14. Spot the pattern, write it with a letter, and it works for every number at once.
➗ Writing a number pattern with letters

Even and odd numbers

Even numbers: 2, 4, 6, 8, … → the nth even number is 2n
Odd numbers: 1, 3, 5, 7, … → the nth odd number is 2n − 1

7th even number = 2 × 7 = 14  ·  10th odd number = 2 × 10 − 1 = 19
💡 Every even number is a multiple of 2, so 2n. Every odd number is one less than an even number, so 2n − 1.
➗ The two sum formulas

Sum of the first n even numbers = n(n + 1)

2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10 = 5 × 6 = 30
2 + 4 + … + 20 (10 terms) = 10 × 11 = 110

Sum of the first n odd numbers = n²

1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 5² = 25
1 + 3 + … up to 7 terms = 7² = 49
💡 Careful: n is the number of terms, not the last number. In 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 there are 5 terms, so n = 5.

One more worth knowing

Sum of the first n natural numbers = n(n + 1)/2

1 + 2 + 3 + … + 10 = (10 × 11)/2 = 55
Before the quiz
Two formulas, easy to swap: odd → n², even → n(n + 1). Test yourself on a small case (1 + 3 = 4 = 2²) whenever you can't remember which is which.
