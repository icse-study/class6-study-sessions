✍️ Writing Practice — answer on paper first (not scored)
1. Both “The Happy Prince” and “Paying the Price” show kindness to poor people. Compare them.
👀 Show model answer
In “The Happy Prince” a statue gives away everything he has, through the swallow, to the poor of his city. In “Paying the Price” a gruff inspector shows mercy to a poor sweeper by sending her home and giving her full pay. Both show that those with power or wealth can make a great difference by acting with compassion.
