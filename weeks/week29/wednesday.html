🌍 Geography · Ch 7 South America · Part 3
Eastern Highlands, Central Lowlands, Coasts & Islands
East of the Andes: ancient highlands with the world's highest waterfall, vast river basins and grasslands, the driest desert, and remarkable islands like the Galapagos.

🪨 Eastern Highlands

Guiana Highlands (north) and Brazilian Highlands (east-central). Part of old Gondwanaland, made of very old rocks like the Deccan Plateau. Rich in minerals such as iron ore and tin.
• Mato Grosso Plateau — western part of the Brazilian Highlands.
• The Paraná and São Francisco rise in the Brazilian Highlands.

💦 Guiana Highlands & Angel Falls

Smaller and lower than the Brazilian Highlands; cover most of Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana. Angel Falls (Orinoco system, Venezuela) plunges from their table-top — world's highest waterfall, 979 m.

🥶 Patagonia

The Plateau of Patagonia in the extreme south lies in the rain shadow of the Andes and has become a cold desert.

🌿 Central Lowlands

Between the Andes and Eastern Highlands; large river basins:
• Amazon Basin — largest. The Amazon (6,437 km) rises in the Andes and enters the Atlantic; largest river by volume. Tributaries: Marañón, Negro, Madeira, Tapajós.
• Orinoco Basin — separated from the Amazon by the Guiana Highlands.
• Paraná–Paraguay Basin — west and south of the Brazilian Highlands; the Uruguay joins the Paraná; combined system = Rio de la Plata, navigable ~1,000 km.
• Pampas — vast grassy lowlands south of the Paraná in Argentina and Uruguay; excellent pastures.

🏖️ Coastal Plains

Narrow coastal plains everywhere except near large river mouths. The west coast has almost none because the Andes rise steeply from the coast. Narrow plains in central Chile, Colombia, Venezuela, north of the Guianas, north and east Brazil, and south of the Brazilian Highlands.
Atacama Desert (north-west Chile) — driest non-polar desert; no vegetation but rich in copper, gold, silver.

🏝️ Islands

Tierra del Fuego — archipelago in the far south, separated by the Strait of Magellan; shared by Chile and Argentina. Cape Horn (Hornos Island) = southernmost point.
Falkland Islands — South Atlantic (~483 km away).
Galapagos Islands — Pacific, off Ecuador; giant tortoises, marine and land iguanas, blue-footed booby, flightless cormorant.

🐊 Knowledge box

Pantanal (Mato Grosso, Brazil) — one of the largest tropical wetlands: jaguars, caimans, capybaras.
Iguazú Falls (Brazil–Argentina) — 275 falls, largest waterfall system.
Lake Maracaibo (Venezuela) — one of Earth's oldest lakes, rich in petroleum.

🎯 Must-remember facts
• Guiana (N, lower) & Brazilian (E-central) Highlands · old Gondwana rocks · iron ore, tin
• Angel Falls 979 m (Venezuela) · Iguazú 275 falls
• Amazon 6,437 km · Andes → Atlantic · largest by volume
• Rio de la Plata = Paraná + Uruguay · Pampas = Argentina & Uruguay
• Atacama driest · Patagonia cold desert · Galapagos off Ecuador
