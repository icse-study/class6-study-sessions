🎲 Fun Corner — relay quiz
1. Where does oxygen first enter the blood?
👀 Show answer
In the alveoli.
2. Which vessel brings the oxygen-rich blood to the heart?
👀 Show answer
The pulmonary veins.
3. Which organelle finally uses the oxygen?
👀 Show answer
Mitochondria.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🫁 The walls of an alveolus and a capillary together are thinner than a sheet of tissue paper.
• 🫀 In a hard sprint, the heart can pump about 4–5 times more blood per minute than at rest.
• 🧗 Mountaineers climbing Everest without extra oxygen train for weeks at height so their blood makes more RBCs.
