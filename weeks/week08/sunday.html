🗣️ ಕನ್ನಡ — quick recall

ಕಿತ್ತೂರು ರಾಣಿ ಚೆನ್ನಮ್ಮ

Queen of Kittur; wife of Raja Mallasarja; fought the British East India Company in 1824 when they refused her adopted heir under the Doctrine of Lapse. Won the first battle, lost the second, died a prisoner at Bailhongal. A symbol of ಸಾಹಸ and ದೇಶಭಕ್ತಿ.

🌍 Geography + 🏛️ History — combined

Water bodies

Five oceans: Pacific (largest), Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic (smallest). A sea is part of an ocean, partly enclosed by land. Longest river: the Nile. Largest inland water body: the Caspian Sea (a lake). Saltiest: the Dead Sea. Water cycle: evaporation → condensation → precipitation → runoff. Tides come from the Moon's gravity.

The Guptas and the State Government

Gupta Empire founded by Chandragupta I (c. 320 CE), capital Pataliputra; Samudragupta the conqueror; Chandragupta II Vikramaditya the patron. Kalidasa in literature, Aryabhata in astronomy, the decimal system and zero in mathematics — the Golden Age.

State government: Vidhan Sabha makes the laws, the Chief Minister and ministers carry them out, and the High Court interprets them.

📚 Key Definitions

Learn these word for word

Equation — a statement that two expressions are equal; it always has an = sign.
Transposition — moving a term across the = sign, which changes its sign.
Ratio — a comparison of two quantities of the same kind, in the same units.
Electric circuit — a closed path along which electricity flows.
Conductor — a material that allows electric current to pass through it.
Air — a mixture of gases surrounding the Earth.
Breathing — the physical act of taking air in and pushing it out.
Respiration — the release of energy from food inside the cells, using oxygen.
Diffusion — the movement of a substance from a higher to a lower concentration.
Sea — a body of salt water smaller than an ocean and partly enclosed by land.
Active voice — the subject performs the action.
Passive voice — the subject receives the action.

📌 Things to Remember
⚠️ Don't confuse these

Maths — equations. Whatever you do to one side, do to the other. Then substitute your answer back to check it.

Maths — area. Never forget the ½ for a triangle, and never use a slanting side as the height.

Maths — profit. Profit % is always worked out on the cost price, never the selling price.

Chemistry. Oxygen supports burning but does not burn. Nitrogen does neither — that is what makes air safe.

Physics. A single cell is a cell; two or more joined together make a battery.

Biology. Breathing is the movement of air; respiration is the chemistry inside the cell. Anaemia is a blood disorder, not a respiratory one.

Biology. Diaphragm down = breathing in, diaphragm up = breathing out.

Geography. The Mediterranean is a sea, and the Caspian is a lake — whatever their names suggest.

History. Chandragupta I of the Guptas (320 CE) is not Chandragupta Maurya (322 BCE).

English. In the passive, the pronoun changes too: “by me”, never “by I”.

French. Cities take à, feminine countries en, masculine countries au, plural countries aux.
