Globe vs map

A globe is a true model of the Earth but is hard to carry and cannot show small areas in detail. A map is a flat drawing of the Earth (or part of it) drawn to scale — easy to carry, fold and study, but slightly distorted.

🧭 The three components of a map

1 · Direction

The four cardinal directions are North, South, East and West. Between them lie the intermediate directions — NE, NW, SE, SW. On most maps, north is at the top. A compass needle always rests pointing north (the north-south magnetic line).

Remember the order clockwise from the top: N → E → S → W — "Never Eat Sour Wheat".

2 · Scale

The scale shows the ratio between the distance on the map and the actual distance on the ground. It lets you measure real distances from a small drawing.

Scale 1 cm = 100 km → 4 cm on the map means 400 km on the ground.

3 · Symbols & colours

Maps use agreed conventional symbols so anyone can read them in any language. The key (or legend) explains them.

⭘ with a dot = capital city · ▪ = town · ══ = railway · ✚ = hospital.

Contour lines

A contour line joins all the places that are at the same height above sea level. Contour lines drawn close together mean a steep slope; lines far apart mean a gentle slope.

💡 Contours never cross each other — one point cannot be at two heights.

Latitudes vs longitudes

Latitudes run east–west, are parallel circles, and get shorter towards the poles. Longitudes (meridians) run north–south from pole to pole and are all of equal length. Together they form the grid used to give any place an exact address.

✍️ English Grammar · Day 2 · Voice
Continuous, Future, Modals & Imperatives
Harder passive structures: continuous tenses ("being"), future & modal verbs ("be"), and command/request sentences. Read the cards, then take the quiz.
⏳ Continuous Tenses — add "being"

The "being" rule

For continuous tenses (verb + -ing), keep the continuous feel by adding being before V₃.

Present continuous: is/am/are + being + V₃ — The artist is drawing a sketch → A sketch is being drawn by the artist.
Past continuous: was/were + being + V₃ — They were cleaning the rooms → The rooms were being cleaned by them.
🔮 Future & Modals — add "be"

The "be" rule

For future (will/shall) or modals (can, must, should, may): will / modal + be + V₃.

The principal will announce the winners → The winners will be announced by the principal.
You must submit the assignment → The assignment must be submitted by you.
💡 Modals always keep a "be" in the passive.
