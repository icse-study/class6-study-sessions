Latitude or longitude?

Latitudes run east–west (like the rungs of a ladder) and are called parallels because they never meet. Longitudes run from pole to pole and are called meridians; they all meet at the poles.

Memory hook: LAT is FLAT. Latitude lines lie flat, side by side.

Reading a map

Every map has a title (what it shows), a scale (map distance vs real ground distance), a direction arrow (usually North) and a legend or key explaining the conventional symbols. A book of maps is an atlas.

Colours: blue = water, green = plains, brown = mountains, yellow = plateaus.

Continents & oceans

Largest continent: Asia. Smallest: Australia. Africa is nicknamed the Dark Continent. Largest ocean: Pacific; smallest and shallowest: Arctic, which surrounds the North Pole.

A / An — it is about SOUND, not spelling

Use an before a vowel sound: an apple, an honest man (the h is silent). Use a before a consonant sound: a boy, a university (it sounds like 'yoo').

Sentence types & punctuation

Declarative states something (.) · Interrogative asks (?) · Imperative commands (.) · Exclamatory shows strong feeling (!).

To make a question from "He is playing", move the helping verb to the front: Is he playing?
