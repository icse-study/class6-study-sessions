🌍 Geography · Ch 7 South America · Part 1
South America — Location, Boundaries & Countries
A continent of lofty peaks, the driest desert, the largest river and dense rainforest. This week: where South America lies, what surrounds it, why it is part of Latin America, and its 12 countries.

📍 Location & size

Fourth largest continent, about 17.8 million sq km. Latitude 13°N to 55°S; longitude 35°W to 81°W. Entirely in the Western Hemisphere; mostly in the Southern Hemisphere. The Equator passes through the north and the Tropic of Capricorn through the south-centre, so most of it lies in the South Torrid Zone. Shape: an elongated triangle tapering south.

🌊 Boundaries

• East: Atlantic Ocean · West: Pacific Ocean · North-west: Caribbean Sea
• Isthmus of Panama (north) links it to North America.
• Drake Passage separates it from Antarctica (named after Sir Francis Drake).
• Cape Horn = southern tip.
• Strait of Magellan separates the mainland from Tierra del Fuego; the most important natural passage between the Atlantic and Pacific.

🔺 A peninsula

Water surrounds it on all sides except the narrow land link in the north, so the continent forms a peninsula — land with water on three sides joined to a mainland on one side.

🗣️ Latin America

South America + Mexico + Central America + the Caribbean = Latin America, because people mostly speak Spanish and Portuguese, languages derived from Latin. The region is famous for American Indian cultures like the Inca of the Andes.

🇧🇷 Political divisions

The mainland has 12 independent countries and French Guiana (an overseas territory of France, capital Cayenne).
• Brazil is the largest — 5th largest country in the world in area and population; borders every country except Ecuador and Chile.
• Argentina is 2nd in size and population.
• Bolivia and Paraguay are the only landlocked countries.
• Brazil = "coffee pot of the world".

🏛️ Capitals

Argentina — Buenos Aires · Bolivia — Sucre · Brazil — Brasília · Chile — Santiago · Colombia — Bogotá · Ecuador — Quito · Guyana — Georgetown · Paraguay — Asunción · Peru — Lima · Suriname — Paramaribo · Uruguay — Montevideo · Venezuela — Caracas.

👥 Uneven population

Lofty mountains, rugged terrain, dense forests and dry deserts discourage settlement. Fertile coastal lands with a favourable climate attract people, so most live near the coasts.

🦙 Wildlife

Green anaconda, python, armadillo, golden lion tamarin, giant anteater, the Andean condor (a large bird of prey) and the llama (camel family; sure-footed, long-haired, survives Andean cold).

🎯 Must-remember facts
• 4th largest · 13°N–55°S · 35°W–81°W · 17.8 million sq km
• Atlantic E, Pacific W, Caribbean NW · Panama N · Drake Passage S
• Strait of Magellan ↔ Tierra del Fuego · Cape Horn = tip
• 12 countries + French Guiana · Brazil largest · Bolivia & Paraguay landlocked
• Latin America = Spanish + Portuguese
🗺️ Map Revision · 15 minutes
Find it on the map
Open your atlas (or an outline map). Point to each place before you open the answer. Say the answer out loud.
1. Mark the Equator and Tropic of Capricorn across South America.
👀 Show model answer
Equator — through Ecuador, Colombia, northern Brazil (near the Amazon mouth); Tropic of Capricorn — through Chile, Argentina, Paraguay, Brazil (near São Paulo).
2. Mark the Drake Passage, Strait of Magellan and Cape Horn.
👀 Show model answer
All at the far south; the Strait cuts between mainland Chile/Argentina and Tierra del Fuego; Cape Horn is on Hornos Island; Drake Passage lies between Cape Horn and Antarctica.
3. Shade the two landlocked countries.
👀 Show model answer
Bolivia and Paraguay.
4. Which two countries do NOT touch Brazil?
👀 Show model answer
Ecuador and Chile.
5. Mark Brasília, Buenos Aires, Lima, Bogotá, Caracas, Santiago.
👀 Show model answer
Brasília — central Brazil; Buenos Aires — on the Rio de la Plata; Lima — Peru coast; Bogotá — Andes of Colombia; Caracas — north coast of Venezuela; Santiago — central Chile.
6. Find French Guiana. Whose territory is it and what is its capital?
👀 Show model answer
Overseas territory of France; capital Cayenne.
