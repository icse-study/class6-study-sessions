➗ Maths — Turning Words into Equations

The translation table

Almost every word problem hides one sentence you can write as an equation. Let the unknown be x.

In wordsAs an equation
The sum of a number and 12 is 25x + 12 = 25
Twice a number is 182x = 18
5 less than a number is 12x − 5 = 12
A number divided by 4 is 6x ÷ 4 = 6
Two consecutive numbersx and x + 1
One part is twice the otherx and 2x

Solving — keep both sides balanced

Whatever you do to one side, do to the other, until x stands alone. Then check by substituting back.

2x − 3 = 15 → add 3 to both sides: 2x = 18 → divide both sides by 2: x = 9. Check: 2(9) − 3 = 15 ✔
🏛️ History — The Vedic Period

The Aryans and the Vedas

The Aryans were Indo-European people who moved into the subcontinent from about 1500 BCE. Their sacred texts, the Vedas, are our chief source for the age.

The four Vedas: Rigveda (the oldest), Samaveda (hymns set to music), Yajurveda (sacrificial formulas) and Atharvaveda (charms and cures). The Ramayana and Mahabharata are epics, NOT Vedas.

Society and economy

Society was divided into four varnas — Brahmins (priests), Kshatriyas (warriors), Vaishyas (traders and farmers) and Shudras (servants) — originally based on occupation, though it later hardened into birth. The economy rested on agriculture and cattle rearing, with trade growing over time.

The Later Vedic period produced the Upanishads and the great epics.

What they do — and how they are funded

Water supply, roads, sanitation, street lights and local development programmes. Their money comes from local taxes AND government grants, not from one source alone.

⚡ ⚗️ Science Recall

Quick reminders before the afternoon quizzes

Physics: S.I. units — metre, kilogram, second, kelvin. Speed = distance ÷ time. Density = mass ÷ volume. A newton is a derived unit.

Chemistry: solids keep shape and volume; liquids keep volume only; gases keep neither and are the only ones easily compressed. Melting, freezing, evaporation and condensation are the changes of state.
