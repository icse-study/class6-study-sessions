🗂️ South America in one card

13°N–55°S · 4th largest · 12 countries + French Guiana · Andes (>7,000 km, Aconcagua 6,959 m) · Eastern Highlands (Guiana, Brazilian) · Central Lowlands (Amazon, Orinoco, Paraná; Pampas) · Coastal Plains (Atacama) · Islands (Tierra del Fuego, Falklands, Galapagos) · Amazon case studies.

🔗 Americas compared

Mountains: Cordilleras (~6,500 km, Denali 6,190 m) vs Andes (>7,000 km, Aconcagua 6,959 m). Old highlands: Canadian Shield & Appalachians vs Brazilian/Guiana Highlands — all very old rocks. Big rivers: Mississippi vs Amazon. Grasslands: Prairies vs Pampas. Deserts: SW USA rain-shadow deserts vs Atacama & Patagonia. Forests: Canadian conifers (lumbering) vs Amazon Selvas.

🔗 Links to other chapters

Agriculture: coffee fazendas & terra roxa (Brazil); Pampas = extensive farming; Amazon = shifting cultivation. Minerals: Atacama copper (Chile, Peru), Maracaibo petroleum, Brazilian Highlands iron ore.

📝 Exam traps

• South America is entirely in the Western but only mostly in the Southern Hemisphere.
• The Andes do not pass through Brazil.
• Angel Falls = highest; Iguazú = largest system.
• Ecuador and Chile do not touch Brazil.
• Drake Passage (to Antarctica) ≠ Strait of Magellan (to Tierra del Fuego).

🎯 Must-remember facts
Draw South America from memory: add 12 countries, Andes, 3 highland areas, 4 rivers, Pampas, Atacama, Patagonia, Titicaca, Angel Falls, 3 island groups, Equator and Tropic of Capricorn.
🗺️ Map Revision · 15 minutes
Find it on the map
Open your atlas (or an outline map). Point to each place before you open the answer. Say the answer out loud.
1. Mark: Caribbean Sea, Atlantic, Pacific, Drake Passage, Strait of Magellan, Isthmus of Panama.
👀 Show model answer
Caribbean — NW; Atlantic — E; Pacific — W; Drake Passage — S (to Antarctica); Strait of Magellan — S (to Tierra del Fuego); Isthmus of Panama — N.
2. Mark rivers Orinoco, Amazon, Paraná, Rio de la Plata.
👀 Show model answer
Orinoco — Venezuela; Amazon — Brazil; Paraná — Brazil/Paraguay/Argentina; Rio de la Plata — Argentina–Uruguay estuary.
3. Mark Andes, Guiana Highlands, Plateau of Patagonia, Selvas, Pampas, Atacama.
👀 Show model answer
Andes — west; Guiana — north; Patagonia — far south; Selvas — Amazon basin; Pampas — Argentina/Uruguay; Atacama — N Chile.
4. Mark Lake Titicaca, Galapagos Islands, Equator, Tropic of Capricorn.
👀 Show model answer
Titicaca — Peru–Bolivia; Galapagos — off Ecuador; Equator — through Ecuador/N Brazil; Capricorn — through N Chile/Argentina, Paraguay, S Brazil.
5. Identify the major river from a map with Colombia, Ecuador, Bolivia, Brazil: its source, mouth and the people living there.
👀 Show model answer
The Amazon: source in the Andes, mouth in the Atlantic Ocean; home of Amerindians who practise hunting, gathering, fishing and shifting cultivation.
