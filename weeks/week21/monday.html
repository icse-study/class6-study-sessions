🔁 Quick recap — Chapter 5

The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn (Mark Twain) — Huck fakes his death, hides on Jackson’s Island, meets runaway Jim, and rafts down the Mississippi. His conscience, trained by society, tells him to turn Jim in; his heart makes him lie to protect his friend.
The Dentist and the Crocodile (Roald Dahl) — a cunning crocodile tries to get a terrified dentist’s head inside his jaws, until a lady reveals he is her harmless pet.

🎭 Devices across the book

Simile: “like Ramachandra” (Sailor) · “light as balloons” (Frost) · “like a light hailstorm” (Butterfly) · “cut me like a knife” (Huck).
Metaphor: “the mountains I raise” (Frost) · “a brilliant kaleidoscope” (Butterfly).
Alliteration: “swift silver wings” (Jewett) · “quivered, quaked” (Dahl).
Personification: the talking crocodile · the garden changing its clothes · the hours that whisper.

✍️ Writing Practice — answer on paper first (not scored)
1. Huck Finn and Ramchand both face a moment that changes them. Compare the two moments.
👀 Show model answer
Ramchand’s moment comes when he cannot answer his mother’s question and his parents respond with silence and honesty; it makes him love them more. Huck’s moment comes when Jim calls him his only true white friend, just as Huck is about to betray him; it makes him choose friendship over the rules he was raised with. Both boys grow because someone treats them with trust and love.
2. Which text in the book did you enjoy most, and why? Refer to the author’s craft.
👀 Show model answer
Model approach: name the text and author; give two reasons (for example, a vivid description, a surprising twist, a character you admired); quote or refer to one line or device; end by saying what the text taught you. Example: “The Dentist and the Crocodile”, because Dahl’s rhyming couplets, movement words and twist ending made me laugh.
