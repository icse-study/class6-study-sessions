✍️ Writing Practice — answer on paper first (not scored)
1. Both Swami and Allarakkhi are unkind to someone who later treats them kindly. What do both stories teach?
👀 Show model answer
Swami is rude to Granny and Allarakkhi insults the inspector, yet Granny forgives Swami and the inspector gives Allarakkhi her full pay. Both characters are left with guilt and regret. The stories teach that kindness can be a powerful lesson, and that we should think before we speak harshly about others.
