🧬 Friday · Week 19 · Chapter 7 (part 2)
Germs on the move 🦠 — and how to stop them 🧼
Communicable (infectious) diseases spread from an infected person to a healthy one when disease-causing microbes — pathogens — get in. This week: the four kinds of pathogen, the diseases they cause, how they travel, and how hygiene blocks them.

7️⃣ Prevention is better than cure

🍲 Hygienic food

Eat fresh seasonal fruits and vegetables · cook cereals and meat well · don't leave cut fruit in the open (flies and germs) · wash vegetables before peeling · keep meat and fish frozen · drink boiled or filtered water from covered containers.

🏘️ Clean surroundings

Keep garbage in covered bins · don't let water stagnate — cover drains (mosquitoes breed in still water) · proper sewer lines to treatment plants · never let faeces or dung reach drinking water.

🎲 Fun Corner
1. Which mosquito gives malaria?
Female, begins with A.
👀 Show answer
The female Anopheles.
2. Why is rabies called hydrophobia?
Hydro = water, phobia = fear.
👀 Show answer
Patients find it painful to swallow and become afraid of water.
3. A child cuts her knee on a muddy farm. Which vaccine does the doctor check?
Locked jaw.
👀 Show answer
Tetanus.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🦟 Only female mosquitoes bite — they need blood to make their eggs. Males drink plant juices.
• 🦴 Dengue is nicknamed "breakbone fever" because of its terrible joint and muscle pain.
• 🧼 Washing hands with soap for 20 seconds — about two rounds of "Happy Birthday" — removes most germs.
• 🇮🇳 The Swachh Bharat Mission was launched on 2 October 2014, Gandhi Jayanti, to make India clean.
