✍️ ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ · Grammar basics

ನಾಮಪದ · Noun

ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿ, ಸ್ಥಳ ಅಥವಾ ವಸ್ತುವಿನ ಹೆಸರನ್ನು ತಿಳಿಸುವ ಪದವೇ ನಾಮಪದ.
A word that names a person, place or thing is a noun.

ಉದಾ: ರಾಮ (person) · ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು (place) · ಪುಸ್ತಕ (thing).
In "ಮಗುವಿಗೆ ಹಾಲು ಇಷ್ಟ." the nouns are ಮಗು (child) and ಹಾಲು (milk).

ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದ · Verb

ಕೆಲಸ ಅಥವಾ ಕ್ರಿಯೆಯನ್ನು ತಿಳಿಸುವ ಪದವೇ ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದ.
A word that tells the work or action being done is a verb.

ಉದಾ: ಓದು (read) · ಆಡು (play) · ನಡೆ (walk) · ತಿನ್ನು (eat).

ವಚನ · Number (singular & plural)

ಏಕವಚನ = singular, ಬಹುವಚನ = plural. For people, the plural is usually made by adding -ರು; for things, by adding -ಗಳು.

ಹುಡುಗ → ಹುಡುಗರು (boy → boys) · ಹುಡುಗಿ → ಹುಡುಗಿಯರು · ಪುಸ್ತಕ → ಪುಸ್ತಕಗಳು · ಮರ → ಮರಗಳು

ಲಿಂಗ · Gender

Kannada has three genders: ಪುಲ್ಲಿಂಗ (masculine), ಸ್ತ್ರೀಲಿಂಗ (feminine) and ನಪುಂಸಕಲಿಂಗ (neuter, for things).

ಅಣ್ಣ (elder brother), ತಂದೆ, ಹುಡುಗ → ಪುಲ್ಲಿಂಗ
ಅಕ್ಕ, ತಾಯಿ, ಹುಡುಗಿ → ಸ್ತ್ರೀಲಿಂಗ
ಮರ, ಪುಸ್ತಕ, ಮನೆ → ನಪುಂಸಕಲಿಂಗ
🗨️ ವಾಕ್ಯ ರಚನೆ · Sentence building

Word order

A Kannada sentence normally runs subject → object → verb, so the verb comes at the end — unlike English.

ಇದು ಒಂದು ಲೇಖನಿ. — This is a pen.
ಅವನು ಶಾಲೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾನೆ. — He goes to school. (ಹೋಗು = go, so ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾನೆ, not ತಿನ್ನುತ್ತಾನೆ = eats.)
ಉದ್ಯಾನದಲ್ಲಿ ಹೂವು ಅರಳಿದೆ. — The flower has bloomed in the garden.
💡 ಅವನು = he · ಅವಳು = she · ಇದು = this · ಅದು = that.

🔄 Review · Landforms of the Earth
Quick review before the quiz!
You studied this on Wednesday. Read through the key points once, then head to the Quiz tab to test yourself. Aim for 6/10 or better!
⛰️ Mountains — 3 Types

Fold Mountains

Tectonic plates collide → crust crumples into folds. Most common type.

Examples: Himalayas · Alps · Andes

Block Mountains

Crust cracks along faults → block is uplifted or surrounding land sinks.

Examples: Vindhyas (India) · Black Forest (Germany) · Vosges (France)

Volcanic Mountains

Built from lava, ash and rocks ejected during volcanic eruptions.

Examples: Mt. Kilimanjaro (Africa) · Mt. Fujiyama (Japan)
🏔️ Valley · 🏜️ Plateau · 🌾 Plains

Rift Valley

Central block slips down between two parallel faults → long, low-lying depression.

Examples: Rhine River Valley · Narmada Valley (India)

Plateau / Tableland

Elevated, broad, flat area rising sharply above surroundings. Rich in minerals.

Examples: Deccan Plateau (India) · Tibet Plateau · East African Plateau

Plains

Vast flat lowland formed by river sediment deposition. Very fertile — high population density.

Examples: Gangetic Plains (India) · Great Plains (North America)
⚡ Forces · 👨‍👩‍👧 People

Endogenic vs Exogenic

Endogenic (internal) — BUILD landforms: plate collision, faulting.
Exogenic (external) — SHAPE landforms: rivers, wind, erosion.

Life in Plains vs Mountains

Plains: High population · easy transport · fertile farming land.
Mountains: Low population · difficult farming · important for tourism & rivers.

Talking about aches — j'ai mal à / aux …

J'ai mal à la tête (I have a headache) · J'ai mal aux genoux (my knees hurt) · J'ai mal aux mains (my hands hurt) · J'ai mal au dos (my back hurts).

💡 à + le → au · à + les → aux · à + la stays à la.

Haddock — Manuel's dog (le chien)

Anne's friends

Catherine — Italian; curly hair (frisés); small eyes (petits yeux); thin and pretty (mince et jolie).
Claire — French (like Anne); long black hair (longs et noirs); blue eyes (bleus); beautiful and very intelligent (belle et très intelligente).

➕ Plurals & Accents

Making sentences plural

Add -s to nouns, change articles (le/la → les), add -s to adjectives, and use the plural verb form.

Singular: Nicolas habite à Lyon. → Plural: Nicolas et Pierre habitent à Lyon.

Clinical vs laboratory thermometer

A clinical thermometer has a narrow range (about 35–42 °C) and a kink that stops the mercury falling back, so the reading can be taken after removing it. A laboratory thermometer has a much wider range (about −10 °C to 110 °C) and no kink.

➗ Maths recap

Area of a rectangle

Area = length × breadth, and the answer is always in square units.

Length 10 m, breadth 6 m → 10 × 6 = 60 m². (Its perimeter would be 2(10 + 6) = 32 m — plain metres, not square metres.)
✍️ English grammar recap

Nouns

A noun names a person, place, animal or thing. In "The boy runs fast in the park", the nouns are boy and park — runs is the verb and fast is an adverb.

Common noun — any one of a class: boy, city, river.
Proper noun — one particular name, always capitalised: Mysuru, Ravi, Kaveri.

Verbs and adjectives

A verb shows an action, an event or a state of being — run, eat, play, is. A descriptive adjective describes or modifies a noun or pronoun — a sweet mango, a tall tree.

💡 Quick test: an adjective answers "what kind?" about a noun; an adverb answers "how?" about a verb.
