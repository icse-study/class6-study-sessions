Spot the trap 🪤
Knowing the science is only half the battle. Many marks are lost to misreading. These are the traps examiners set most often.
TrapExampleHow to beat it
NOT / EXCEPT"Which is NOT a function of blood?"circle the word; find the one that doesn't fit
always / only / never / all"All arteries carry oxygen-rich blood"one exception makes it false (pulmonary artery)
look-alike optionspulmonary artery vs pulmonary veincheck the direction words carefully
right fact, wrong questionasked "where is bile MADE" → gall bladder is temptingmade in liver, stored in gall bladder
two-step questions"The enzyme that acts in the organ after the oesophagus"do step 1 (stomach), then step 2 (pepsin)
numbers20 vs 32 teeth; 3 vs 2 lobesask: child or adult? right or left?
🎯 Exam Trivia
• 🪤 In many tests, the most common wrong answer is a TRUE fact that answers a DIFFERENT question.
• 🔎 Underlining key words while reading can cut misreading errors sharply.
• ⏱️ Rushing the easy questions causes more lost marks than getting stuck on hard ones.
