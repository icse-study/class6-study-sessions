The Cell — the building block of life 🧱
A banyan tree, an elephant and you all began life as a single cell. The cell is the structural and functional unit of all living things. The word comes from Latin cella, a small room. The study of cells is cytology.

1️⃣ Discovery of the cell
ScientistWhat he did
Robert Hooke (1665)looked at a thin slice of cork through his two-lens microscope, saw box-like compartments and named them cells. They were dead and empty.
Antonie van Leeuwenhoekbuilt a simple microscope with a single lens and was the first to see living cells (e.g. blood cells in a frog's foot-web), but didn't call them cells

Living or dead?

A cell is living when it contains protoplasm — the living substance, made of cytoplasm + nucleus. School labs use a compound microscope (eyepiece, low- and high-power objectives, stage, clips, coarse and fine adjustment knobs, light source).

4️⃣ Sizes

From giant to tiny

🥚 Largest: the ostrich egg · 🧵 Longest: nerve cells (up to about 3 metres in large animals) · 🦠 Smallest: bacteria, 0.2–0.5 micrometre. (1 micrometre = one-thousandth of a millimetre.)

5️⃣ The cell theory

Schleiden, Schwann and Virchow

1. Every living organism is made of one or more cells. 2. The cell is the structural unit of life. 3. The cell is the functional unit of life. 4. All cells arise from pre-existing cells.

7️⃣ Parts of a cell

🔬 Animal cell and plant cell

Both have a nucleus, cytoplasm, cell membrane and mitochondria. Only plant cells have a cell wall, plastids and one large vacuole.
PartFacts
Cell membrane (plasma membrane)thin, flexible, living; in plant and animal cells; selectively permeable — lets only some substances in and out
Cell wallonly in plant cells, outside the membrane; made of cellulose; non-living; gives shape and rigidity; freely permeable
Cytoplasmsemi-liquid, colourless jelly between membrane and nucleus; holds the organelles
Nucleuscontrols all activities of the cell; important in cell division

🎯 Inside the nucleus

Humans have 46 chromosomes (23 pairs) in each body cell; a pea plant has 14 (7 pairs). Genes on chromosomes pass characters from parents to offspring.
8️⃣ Organelles — the cell's little organs

Seen with an electron microscope

Endoplasmic reticulum · ribosomes · mitochondria (release energy — the "powerhouse") · Golgi bodies · lysosomes · centrosome (animal cells) · plastids (plant cells).

PlastidColourJob
Chloroplastgreentraps sunlight for photosynthesis
Chromoplastyellow, redcolours flowers and fruits to attract insects
Leucoplastcolourlessstores starch, fats and proteins (in seeds)

Vacuoles

Non-living spaces filled with cell sap (water + dissolved substances). Plant cells: few and large. Animal cells: many and small.

🔟 Cell division

Why cells divide

New cells are needed for growth, repair, replacement of old cells and reproduction.

Made of cellulose.
👀 Show answer

3. What gives a tomato its red colour?

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🧍 Your body has about 37 trillion cells — and tens of thousands of skin cells flake off every minute, to be replaced by new ones.
• 🥚 A hen's egg yolk is a single cell — you've eaten a giant cell for breakfast!
• 🔍 Leeuwenhoek made his own tiny lenses; some of his microscopes magnified about 270 times.
• 🧵 A giraffe has nerve cells that run several metres down its long neck and legs.

Sentences come in three shapes 🧩
A sentence can have one idea, two ideas joined together, or a main idea with a helper idea hanging on it.
TypeWhat it isExample
ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ವಾಕ್ಯ (simple)one complete verb, one ideaಹಕ್ಕಿ ಹಣ್ಣನ್ನು ತಿನ್ನುತ್ತದೆ. · ಮೀನು ನೀರಲ್ಲಿ ಈಜುತ್ತದೆ.
ಸಂಯೋಜಿತ ವಾಕ್ಯ (compound)two simple sentences joined by ಮತ್ತು, ಆದರೆ, ಆದ್ದರಿಂದಮಕ್ಕಳು ಹಾಲನ್ನು ಕುಡಿಯುತ್ತಾರೆ ಮತ್ತು ಹಣ್ಣನ್ನೂ ತಿನ್ನುತ್ತಾರೆ.
ಮಿಶ್ರ ವಾಕ್ಯ (complex)a main clause (ಪ್ರಧಾನ ವಾಕ್ಯ) + a dependent clause (ಅಧೀನ ವಾಕ್ಯ)ತಮ್ಮ ಗ್ರಾಮಕ್ಕೆ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಬಸ್ಸು ಬರುವುದೆಂದು ತಿಳಿದು ಗ್ರಾಮಸ್ಥರು ಸಂತಸಪಟ್ಟರು.

Spot the difference

ಸಂಯೋಜಿತ: both parts could stand alone — join them with ಮತ್ತು / ಆದರೆ. ಮಿಶ್ರ: one part cannot stand alone ("ಕುವೆಂಪು ಅವರ ಗೀತೆಗಳನ್ನು ಕೇಳಿ…" needs the rest: "…ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳು ಮೈಮರೆತರು").

💡 Joining with ಆದರೆ: "ಬೆಕ್ಕು ಮನೆಯೊಳಗೆ ಬಂದಿತು ಆದರೆ ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಹಾಲಿರಲಿಲ್ಲ." → ಸಂಯೋಜಿತ.
👯 ಜೋಡುನುಡಿ — word pairs

Words that love company

Two related words used together for rhythm and emphasis: ನೆರೆ-ಹೊರೆ (neighbours) · ರಾತ್ರಿ-ಹಗಲು (night and day) · ಉಡುಗೆ-ತೊಡುಗೆ (clothes and ornaments) · ಆಟ-ಪಾಠ (play and study) · ಕಷ್ಟ-ಕಾರ್ಪಣ್ಯ (hardships) · ಗಿಡ-ಮರ (plants and trees) · ಹಣ್ಣು-ಹಂಪಲು (fruits) · ಬಂಧು-ಬಳಗ (relatives) · ತಿಂಡಿ-ತಿನಿಸು (snacks)
