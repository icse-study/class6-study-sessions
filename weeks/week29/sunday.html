Eastern Highlands, Central Lowlands, Coasts & Islands — think and write
Wednesday's topic, one level deeper. Write each answer in your notebook first (4–6 lines), then open the model answer and compare. Then take the 10-question Geo Quiz.
1. Differentiate between the Guiana Highlands and the Brazilian Highlands.
👀 Show model answer
Guiana: in the north (Guyana, Suriname, French Guiana, Venezuela); smaller and lower; Angel Falls. Brazilian: east-central; larger and higher; includes Mato Grosso; source of Paraná and São Francisco. Both: old Gondwana rocks rich in iron ore and tin.
2. State two notable features of the Eastern Highlands.
👀 Show model answer
1. Made of very old hard rocks of Gondwanaland (like the Deccan), rich in iron ore and tin. 2. Source of major rivers (Paraná, São Francisco) and home of Angel Falls, the world's highest waterfall.
3. Describe the Central Lowlands of South America.
👀 Show model answer
Between the Andes and Eastern Highlands. Includes the Amazon Basin (largest; Amazon 6,437 km, largest by volume), the Orinoco Basin, and the Paraná–Paraguay Basin (Rio de la Plata, navigable 1,000 km) with the Pampas grasslands of Argentina and Uruguay.
4. Why is the west coast of South America almost devoid of coastal plains?
👀 Show model answer
The Andes rise steeply right from the Pacific coast, leaving no room for a plain.
5. Write notes on: Atacama Desert, Plateau of Patagonia.
👀 Show model answer
Atacama: north-west Chile; driest non-polar desert; no vegetation; rich in copper, gold, silver. Patagonia: extreme south (Argentina); in the rain shadow of the Andes; a cold desert.
6. Describe the islands of South America.
👀 Show model answer
Tierra del Fuego — archipelago in the far south across the Strait of Magellan, shared by Chile and Argentina; Cape Horn on Hornos Island. Falkland Islands — South Atlantic. Galapagos — Pacific, off Ecuador; giant tortoises, iguanas, blue-footed boobies.
7. Name the source and mouth of: Amazon, Orinoco, Paraná, Uruguay.
👀 Show model answer
Amazon — Andes → Atlantic. Orinoco — Guiana Highlands → Atlantic. Paraná — Brazilian Highlands → Atlantic (via Rio de la Plata). Uruguay — Brazilian Highlands → joins Paraná → Atlantic.
8. What is the Pantanal? Name three animals found there.
👀 Show model answer
One of the world's largest tropical wetlands, mainly in western Brazil (Mato Grosso). Animals: jaguars, caimans, capybaras (and hundreds of bird species).
9. Compare Angel Falls and Iguazú Falls.
👀 Show model answer
Angel Falls: Venezuela, Orinoco system, world's highest single waterfall (979 m) from the Guiana Highlands. Iguazú: Brazil–Argentina border, 275 falls on the Iguazú River — the largest waterfall system.
10. How do physical features influence the activities of people? Compare the Amazon lowlands and the Pampas.
👀 Show model answer
Amazon: hot, wet, dense forest — people hunt, gather, fish and practise shifting cultivation; few roads; sparse population. Pampas: flat, fertile grasslands — large cattle ranches and wheat farms, towns and railways, denser settlement.
