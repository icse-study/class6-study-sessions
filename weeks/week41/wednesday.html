📖 Story time
Arunima Sinha climbs back
In 2011, national volleyball player Arunima Sinha lost her leg after being pushed from a moving train. In hospital she decided to climb Everest. Training with Bachendri Pal, the first Indian woman on Everest, she reached the summit on 21 May 2013 — the first female amputee to do so — and went on to climb the highest peaks of many continents, including Kilimanjaro and Aconcagua, peaks you studied this year.
📖 Story time
The volcano that made a new island
In November 1963, fishermen off Iceland saw smoke rising from the sea. A volcano was erupting from the sea floor, and within days a brand-new island, Surtsey, was born. Scientists have watched seeds, insects and birds arrive on it ever since — a living lesson on how land forms. Next year you'll learn exactly how volcanoes like this work!
🤔 Did you know? Trivia corner
• The deepest hole humans have drilled (Kola, Russia) is about 12 km — not even through the crust.
• Earth's inner core is about as hot as the surface of the Sun.
• Lightning strikes Earth about 100 times every second.
• Mt Everest's summit rock is marine limestone — once a sea floor!
