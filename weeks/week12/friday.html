Finishing the job — the intestines 🌀
Chyme leaves the stomach and enters the small intestine, where digestion is completed and food is absorbed. Then the large intestine saves water and removes the waste.

🗺️ Today's route

1️⃣ Small intestine · 2️⃣ Liver & bile · 3️⃣ Pancreas · 4️⃣ Intestinal juice · 5️⃣ End-products · 6️⃣ Villi & absorption · 7️⃣ Assimilation · 8️⃣ Large intestine · 🎲 Fun Corner

1️⃣ The small intestine

Long, narrow and coiled

About 6–7 metres long, coiled to fit in the belly. Three parts: duodenum (C-shaped first part) → jejunum → ileum. Juices from the liver and pancreas enter the duodenum.

💡 "Small" refers to its width — it is about four times longer than the large intestine!
2️⃣ The liver and bile

The largest gland

The liver (upper right of the abdomen) is the largest gland in the body. It makes greenish-yellow bile, stored in the gall bladder and poured into the duodenum.

Bile has no enzymes. It emulsifies fats — breaks big fat globules into tiny droplets, just as washing-up liquid breaks up grease — so lipase can work on them. It also makes the food alkaline.

6️⃣ Villi — the absorbing fingers

Millions of tiny fingers

The inner wall of the ileum is covered with millions of finger-like villi. They hugely increase the surface area for absorption. Each villus has a network of blood capillaries and a central lymph vessel (lacteal).

7️⃣ Assimilation

Using the food

Blood carries absorbed food to every cell. Glucose is burnt for energy (respiration); amino acids build new cells and repair tissues; fats are stored as reserve energy. Extra glucose is stored as glycogen in the liver and muscles, or turned into fat.

8️⃣ The large intestine

The water saver

About 1.5 m long and wider. Parts: caecum (with the small appendix) → colon → rectum → anus. It makes no enzymes. It absorbs water and some salts from the waste. The semi-solid faeces are stored in the rectum and passed out through the anus — egestion.

💡 Fibre (roughage) in fruits and vegetables helps the waste move smoothly and prevents constipation.
🎲 Fun Corner
1. Bile has no enzyme. So how does it help?
Think washing-up liquid.
👀 Show answer
It emulsifies fat into tiny droplets so lipase can work faster.
2. Where is digestion completed?
Last part of the small intestine.
👀 Show answer
The ileum.
3. Name the end-product of protein digestion.
👀 Show answer
Amino acids.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🎾 Unfolded, the lining of the small intestine covers about 30 square metres — roughly half a badminton court.
• 🟢 Bile is why the body's waste is brown: its pigments change colour on the way through.
• 🦠 Trillions of helpful bacteria live in the large intestine; some even make vitamin K for us.
• ♻️ The liver can regrow: even if part of it is removed, it can grow back to nearly full size.
