⚗️ Chemistry — Matter & Separation

The three states

Solid — fixed shape AND fixed volume; particles tightly packed. Liquid — fixed volume but takes the container's shape. Gas — neither fixed shape nor fixed volume; particles far apart, so gases are highly compressible.

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space.
MethodUsed to separate
FiltrationInsoluble solid from a liquid (tea leaves from tea)
SedimentationHeavy insoluble particles settling to the bottom
DecantationPouring off the clear liquid after sedimentation
EvaporationA dissolved solid from its liquid (salt from sea water)
SublimationA solid that turns straight into gas (camphor, ammonium chloride)

Changes of state

Melting (solid→liquid) · Freezing (liquid→solid) · Evaporation & boiling (liquid→gas) · Condensation (gas→liquid) · Sublimation (solid→gas directly).

Water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C. Water is called the universal solvent; its formula is H₂O. Air is a mixture, not a pure substance.
