## How to fill this in (read once)

**You write content. I handle all HTML, ids, quiz wiring and sync code.**

- **Chapter / topic** is the one field I can't guess. Without it I have to invent a topic, which risks missing what was actually taught. If a slot has no chapter, write `SKIP` and I'll leave that slot alone.
- **Study material** — bullets, paste from a PDF/textbook, tables, definitions, formulas, whatever. Rough is fine; I rewrite it into study cards. More detail = better distractors in the quiz.
- **Practice questions** — any format: MCQ, fill-in-the-blank, true/false, short answer, numericals. I convert everything to 4-option MCQs with explanations. **Give answers where you know them** — otherwise I answer from the study material and you should spot-check.
- **Question count** — default is **15 scored MCQs** per subject (10 core + 5 marked 🔴 Hard). Override per slot if you want a different number. Pass mark is always 60%.
- **Open-ended questions** (define X, explain why, write 3 sentences) don't fit the MCQ engine — I keep those as *unscored* written prompts with collapsible model answers in the study tab. Mark them `[WRITE]` if you want to force that.
- **Diagrams / images** — I can't embed pictures. Describe them in words (labels, parts) and I'll build a labelling-style question instead.

**Defaults I apply unless you say otherwise:** hard/exam-style questions, Class 6 ICSE level, tricky-but-fair distractors, an explanation on every question, one study tab + one quiz tab per subject.
