The lotus effect

Water drops roll off lotus leaves, taking dust with them — the leaf cleans itself. Scientists copy this to make self-cleaning paints and fabrics.

• 🐅 The Sundarbans mangroves, home of the Royal Bengal tiger, are the largest mangrove forest in the world.
• 💧 Water hyacinth spreads so fast it can double its area in about two weeks, choking lakes.
