Know your enemy 🦟💧
Habitat and adaptation meet disease: the mosquito is perfectly adapted to still water. Understand its life cycle and you can stop malaria, dengue and filariasis.

🔁 Life cycle — four stages
StageWhereAdaptation
1. egglaid on still waterfloats in groups or singly
2. larva ("wriggler")in waterhangs at the surface and breathes air through a tube
3. pupa ("tumbler")in waterdoes not eat; changes into an adult
4. adultairfemale sucks blood to make eggs; male drinks plant juice

Egg to adult in about a week

In warm weather the whole cycle takes about 7–10 days — so a forgotten cooler can produce hundreds of mosquitoes in a fortnight.

🚫 Break the cycle
Stage attackedMethod
eggs, larvaeempty coolers, pots, tyres weekly; cover tanks; fill potholes
larvaespread a thin film of oil (it blocks their breathing tubes); release larvivorous fish such as Gambusia and guppy
adultsnets, screens, repellents, full sleeves
