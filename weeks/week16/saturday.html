🐟 External features of a fish

Paired fins come in twos, one on each side (shoulder, hip). Unpaired (median) fins run along the middle line (dorsal, ventral, tail).

🌿 Pollination in Vallisneria

Male and female flowers grow on different plants. Male flowers break free, float, and pollinate the female flower held up at the surface.
🔬 Experiment — cactus vs leafy plant

Set-up

Cover one shoot of a leafy potted plant and one shoot of a small cactus with clear plastic bags. Keep both in sunlight for an hour or more.
👀 Result: the leafy plant's bag has many more droplets.
✅ Conclusion: a cactus loses very little water — its spines and waxy stem cut transpiration.

🕵️ Habitat detective
Case 1. A plant has hollow light stems, thin ribbon leaves and tiny roots.
Where does it live?
👀 Show answer
Submerged in water — like Vallisneria.
Case 2. An animal has thick fur even on its toes and strong hooves.
👀 Show answer
A mountain animal — the mountain goat.
Case 3. An animal has hollow bones, a streamlined body and big breast muscles.
👀 Show answer
An aerial animal — a bird.
Case 4. A plant has spines, a thick waxy stem and very long roots.
👀 Show answer
A desert plant — cactus.
Case 5. A tree is cone-shaped with needle-like leaves.
👀 Show answer
A mountain tree — pine or fir.
