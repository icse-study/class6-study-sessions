Everything that lives, respires 🌬️
Humans breathe with lungs. But a mango tree, an earthworm, a fish and a yeast cell also release energy from food. How do they get their oxygen?

3️⃣ Plants respire too

Day and night

Every living plant cell respires all the time. Leaves take in oxygen through stomata, stems through small pores called lenticels, and roots from air spaces in the soil.

💡 In bright light, photosynthesis is faster than respiration, so a plant gives out more oxygen than it uses. At night it only respires.

Waterlogged roots

If a field is flooded for many days, water fills the air spaces in the soil. Roots cannot get oxygen and the plant may die.

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🪱 After heavy rain, earthworms come to the surface — the soil is so full of water that they struggle to get oxygen.
• 🐸 Some frogs can take in enough oxygen through their skin to stay under water for hours.
• 🍷 Yeast's anaerobic respiration makes bread rise and is used to make alcohol fuel from sugarcane molasses.
