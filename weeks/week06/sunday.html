📌 Things to Remember
⚠️ Don't confuse these

Maths — signs. Adding rules and multiplying rules are different. (−5) + (−3) = −8, but (−5) × (−3) = +15.

Maths — proportion. More workers means LESS time. That is inverse, so multiply first to get the total work.

Maths — units. Perimeter is in cm; area is in cm²; volume is in cm³.

Science — cells. Plant cells have a cell wall and chloroplasts; animal cells have neither.

Science — density. Always mass ÷ volume, never the other way round.

Geography — scale. Map to ground, multiply. Ground to map, divide. A river is a water body, not a landform.

English — tenses. After 'will', the verb stays in its base form: "will go", never "will went".

French. The endings ‑e, ‑es and ‑ent all sound the same, so only the spelling separates them.

Python. input() always returns a string, and "5" + "3" gives "53", not 8.
