Examiner's checklist

Pencil only · diagram at least a third of a page · label lines ruled, parallel, never crossing · labels in one column on one side · a title underneath, e.g. "L.S. of a flower".

🎯 Diagram Trivia
• 📏 "L.S." means longitudinal section — cut from top to bottom. "T.S." means transverse section — cut across.
• 🌺 In many flowers the stigma sits higher than the anthers, which makes self-pollination harder.
• 🫘 The radicle points to the micropyle so that it can push out through the same weak spot where water entered.
