The Circulatory System — the body's delivery network ❤️
You've learnt how food is digested and oxygen is breathed in. But how do they reach your toes? The circulatory (cardiovascular) system — blood, blood vessels and the heart — carries them everywhere and takes wastes away.

1️⃣ Blood

Two parts

Plasma — the pale yellow liquid part: about 90% water and 10% dissolved nutrients, proteins, wastes and hormones. Salts such as sodium chloride make blood taste salty. Corpuscles — the blood cells. An adult has about 4.5–5.5 litres of blood.

Dengue and platelets

In dengue (and purpura), platelets can fall to just 25–30 thousand per cubic mm, and the body starts bleeding.

3️⃣ Jobs of blood

Seven jobs

1. carries digested food from the small intestine · 2. carries O₂ to cells and CO₂ to the lungs · 3. takes wastes to the kidneys · 4. keeps water balance · 5. spreads heat evenly · 6. immunity · 7. clotting.

Capillaries

Very narrow vessels whose walls are only one cell thick (endothelium). They join arteries to veins. Food, gases and wastes are exchanged through their walls.

5️⃣ The heart

The pump

Cone-shaped, about the size of your clenched fist, between the lungs, tilted slightly to the left, protected by the ribcage. Made of cardiac muscle, which works without rest all your life.

Right and left

A wall, the septum, divides the heart. The right side handles deoxygenated blood; the left side handles oxygenated blood — they never mix. AV valves between auricles and ventricles keep blood moving one way.

6️⃣ Two circuits

Pulmonary circulation (heart ⇄ lungs)

right auricle → right ventricle → pulmonary artery → lungs (blood picks up O₂) → pulmonary veins → left auricle

Systemic circulation (heart ⇄ body)

left auricle → left ventricle → aorta → body → venae cavae → right auricle

💡 Blood passes through the heart twice on each full trip round the body.

8️⃣ Blood groups

A, B, AB and O

Discovered by Karl Landsteiner (1900), based on antigens (proteins) on RBCs. A has antigen A · B has B · AB has both · O has neither.
O = universal donor · AB = universal recipient.

💡 A donor usually gives about one pint (420 mL). A blood bank stores donated blood.
9️⃣ Keeping the heart healthy

Heart-smart habits

🏃 exercise and play every day · 🥗 plenty of vegetables, fruits and fibre (whole grains, oats) · 🍟 less fried and oily food · 🍬 less sugar (obesity strains the heart) · 🍔 fewer fast foods.

🎲 Fun Corner
1. Which artery carries deoxygenated blood?
The odd one.
👀 Show answer
The pulmonary artery — to the lungs.
2. Why does the left ventricle have the thickest wall?
How far must it pump?
👀 Show answer
It pumps blood to the whole body, so it needs the most force.
3. Which blood group can give blood to everyone?
👀 Show answer
O — universal donor.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🔄 More than 2 million red blood cells are destroyed every second — and replaced just as fast by the bone marrow.
• 🌍 Laid end to end, all your blood vessels would stretch around 1 lakh km — more than twice round the Earth.
• 🐋 A blue whale's heart is about the size of a small car.
• 💓 Your heart beats about 1 lakh times a day without ever taking a break.
