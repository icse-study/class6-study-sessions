🧱 Tissues and transport (weeks 4–5)

A tissue

A group of similar cells doing one job. In plants: xylem carries water and minerals up from the roots; phloem carries food made in the leaves to all parts.

💡 Strip a ring of bark from a tree (this cuts the phloem): food can't reach the roots, the bark above the ring swells, and in time the roots starve.

🎯 Fun Trivia — the year's best
• 🦇 Bats are the only mammals that can truly fly (week 2).
• 🥚 An egg yolk is a single cell — the ostrich egg is the biggest (week 14).
• 🦴 A peepal leaf soaked for weeks leaves a lacy skeleton of veins (week 9).
• 🍏 The apple you eat is swollen flower base, not ovary (week 10).
• 🌳 A big tree can pull hundreds of litres of water up its trunk on a hot day — by transpiration, with no pump at all (weeks 5 and 9).
