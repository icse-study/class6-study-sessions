3️⃣ First aid for bleeding

Stop the flow

1. Press firmly on the cut with a clean cloth. 2. Raise the injured part above the heart. 3. Keep pressing until bleeding stops; then bandage. 4. Get help for deep cuts.

💡 Platelets form the clot; pressing helps them do it.
4️⃣ Blood donation

Give the gift of life

Healthy adults (usually 18–65 years, over about 45–50 kg) can donate about one pint (350–450 mL). The body replaces the fluid within a day and the cells within weeks. Blood is stored in a blood bank.

Before a transfusion, the donor's and patient's blood groups are matched. O can be given to all; AB can receive from all.
🎲 Fun Corner — true or myth?
1. "Donating blood makes you weak for months."
👀 Show answer
Myth — fluid returns within a day, cells within weeks.
2. "Exercise makes the heart stronger."
👀 Show answer
True — the heart is a muscle.
3. "Only old people get high blood pressure."
👀 Show answer
Myth — poor diet and no exercise can raise it in the young too.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🫀 Your heart pumps about 7000 litres of blood every day.
• 🩸 World Blood Donor Day is on 14 June, Karl Landsteiner's birthday.
• 🏃 Regular exercise can lower the resting heart rate, because a stronger heart pumps more blood with each beat.
