Two names to remember

Robert Hooke first saw and named cells in 1665, looking at a slice of cork. The cell is the structural and functional unit of life.

🗣️ Kannada — ವಚನ & ಲಿಂಗ

ವಚನಗಳು — number

ಏಕವಚನ = singular, ಬಹುವಚನ = plural. Things usually add ‑ಗಳು (ಮನೆ → ಮನೆಗಳು, ಪುಸ್ತಕ → ಪುಸ್ತಕಗಳು) while people usually add ‑ರು (ಹುಡುಗ → ಹುಡುಗರು).

ಲಿಂಗಗಳು — gender

ಪುಲ್ಲಿಂಗ masculine · ಸ್ತ್ರೀಲಿಂಗ feminine · ನಪುಂಸಕ ಲಿಂಗ neuter (things such as ಮರ, ಮನೆ, ಪುಸ್ತಕ).

Pairs: ಅಣ್ಣ–ಅಕ್ಕ · ಹುಡುಗ–ಹುಡುಗಿ · ರಾಜ–ರಾಣಿ · ತಂದೆ–ತಾಯಿ. A name such as ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು is an ಅಂಕಿತ ನಾಮ (proper noun).
🧬 Biology — The Cell & Plant Life

From cell to plant

A group of similar cells doing one job is a tissue. In plants, xylem carries water and minerals upward and phloem carries food made in the leaves.

Root systems: taproot (one main root, e.g. mango, carrot) and fibrous (many thin roots, e.g. grass, wheat).

Leaves and flowers

Venation is the pattern of veins: reticulate (net-like — hibiscus, mango) and parallel (grass, maize, banana). Photosynthesis uses sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make food, releasing oxygen. Transpiration is the loss of water vapour from the leaves.

Flower parts: stamens (male) and pistil/carpel (female). Pollination is the transfer of pollen from anther to stigma; the ovary later becomes the fruit.
