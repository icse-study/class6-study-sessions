Canadian Shield, Central Plains & Eastern Highlands
East of the Rockies lie three very different lands: the ancient Canadian Shield with its thousands of lakes, the vast Central Plains, and the old, worn Eastern Highlands.

🪨 The Canadian Shield

The northern region around Hudson Bay up to the Great Lakes. Made of some of the oldest rocks in the world, resembling the rocks of India's Deccan Plateau.

💡 Shield = a stable block of land of old hard rocks.

🧊 Ice Age & the Great Lakes

In the recent geological past (Ice Age), thick ice sheets covered the Shield and reached the Great Lakes. Denudation by ice sheets scooped out many basins, which now hold lakes — including the five Great Lakes: Superior, Michigan, Huron, Erie, Ontario.

💡 HOMES = Huron, Ontario, Michigan, Erie, Superior.

🌾 The Central (Great) Plains

Extend from the Arctic coast to the Gulf of Mexico. Undulating lowlands formed by denudation by glaciers and rivers.
• South of the Great Lakes: a vast lowland drained by the Mississippi and its tributaries; it widens southward and merges with the Gulf Coast Plain.
• North of the Great Lakes: drained by rivers flowing to Hudson Bay and the Arctic Ocean.

🏞️ The Eastern Highlands

Include the Appalachian Range and the Labrador Plateau.
• Appalachians: old fold mountains, denuded over millions of years; peaks do not exceed 2,100 m.
• East of them: Piedmont Plateau and Atlantic Coastal Plain.
• The St Lawrence Valley separates the Labrador Plateau from the Appalachians.

📖 Piedmont

Piedmont plateaus lie at the foot of mountains, with highlands on one side and flat land or sea on the other. (Pied = foot, mont = mountain.)

⚖️ West vs East

Western Cordilleras: young, high (Denali 6,190 m), rugged, volcanic.
Eastern Highlands: old, worn down, low (< 2,100 m), no active volcanoes.

🎯 Must-remember facts
• Shield: Hudson Bay → Great Lakes · oldest rocks · like Deccan
• Ice sheets scooped basins → lakes incl. 5 Great Lakes (HOMES)
• Central Plains: Arctic → Gulf of Mexico · Mississippi south of lakes
• Appalachians: old fold, < 2,100 m · Labrador Plateau · Piedmont · Atlantic Coastal Plain
• St Lawrence Valley separates Labrador Plateau & Appalachians
