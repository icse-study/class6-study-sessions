Design rule

Every feature has a cost. Thick fur is great in Ladakh but deadly in the Thar. Good design fits the habitat.

🎲 Fun Corner — design challenges
1. Design a plant for a salty, flooded mud-flat.
👀 Show answer
Breathing roots that stick up out of the mud (like mangroves).
2. Design a mammal for icy seas.
👀 Show answer
Streamlined body, flippers, thick blubber, lungs with big breath-holding ability.
3. Design a seed to cross a river.
👀 Show answer
Light, air-filled, waterproof coat — it floats.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🦆 Ducks have webbed feet for swimming and oily feathers that keep them dry.
• 🦎 Geckos can walk on ceilings thanks to millions of tiny hairs on their toes.
• ✈️ Engineers copied bird wing shapes and fish body shapes when designing aircraft and submarines.
