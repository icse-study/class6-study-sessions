🧬 Saturday · Week 29 · Term review B
Pack your survival guide 🎒
You are going on four school trips. For each place, learn how the local plants and animals survive — and what YOU must do to stay healthy there.
🏜️ Trip 1: Thar Desert, Rajasthan
Locals survive by…You stay healthy by…
camel: fat hump, thick urine, pad feetdrinking lots of water — avoid dehydration
cactus: spines, waxy stemhat and shade — avoid heat stroke and sunburn
desert rats: burrows by dayresting in the hottest hours
🏔️ Trip 2: Ladakh (high mountains)
Locals survive by…You stay healthy by…
yak and mountain goat: thick fur, more RBCsresting on day one so your body adjusts to thin air
pine and fir: needle leaves, cone shapewarm layers; avoid frostbite
some animals hibernateeating well — cold burns more energy
🌊 Trip 3: Sundarbans (mangrove wetland)
Locals survive by…You stay healthy by…
mangroves: breathing rootsnets and repellent — mosquitoes spread malaria
fish: gills, fins, swim bladderonly boiled or bottled water — avoid cholera and typhoid
🏙️ Trip 4: a big city
Watch out for…You stay healthy by…
crowds → flu, TB dropletsmasks when ill, covering coughs, BCG vaccine
stagnant water → dengueno open containers of water
fast food → obesitybalanced meals with fruit and vegetables
dust and smoke → asthma, allergiesavoid smoke; inhaler if prescribed

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🏔️ Ladakh's Khardung La road is one of the highest motorable passes in the world — at over 5000 m, each breath has about half the oxygen of a breath at sea level.
• 🐯 Tigers in the Sundarbans swim between islands and even drink slightly salty water.
• 🏜️ Desert sand can heat up to over 60 °C in summer — hot enough to cook an egg.
