🕵️ Health inspector — what went wrong?
Case 1. Many people in a colony get cholera after the monsoon. The water tank is near a leaking sewer.
Route?
👀 Show answer
Drinking water was contaminated with sewage. Boil or filter water; repair the sewer.
Case 2. A school has old tyres and coolers full of rainwater. Three children get dengue.
Breeding ground.
👀 Show answer
Stagnant water breeds Aedes. Empty tyres and coolers every week.
Case 3. A family eats half-cooked pork at a picnic. Weeks later one member has stomach pain and worm segments in the stool.
👀 Show answer
Taeniasis (tapeworm). Always cook meat thoroughly.
Case 4. A boy shares his towel with a friend who has red, sticky eyes.
👀 Show answer
Conjunctivitis may spread. Never share towels or handkerchiefs.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🧪 In 1854, Dr John Snow stopped a cholera outbreak in London by removing the handle of one water pump — proving cholera spreads through water.
• 🇮🇳 Ronald Ross discovered in 1897, working in India, that mosquitoes carry malaria. He won the Nobel Prize in 1902.
• 💉 Louis Pasteur made the first rabies vaccine in 1885 and saved a boy bitten by a rabid dog.
