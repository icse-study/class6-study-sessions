Units — the mark-losing trap

Perimeter is a length, so it is measured in cm, m, km. Area covers a surface, so it is measured in square units: cm², m².

Fencing, running around a field, or bending a wire → perimeter. Carpeting, levelling, or painting a floor → area.

Working backwards

If you are given the answer and asked for a side, reverse the formula. Perimeter of a square is 48 cm → side = 48 ÷ 4 = 12 cm. Area of a rectangle is 96 cm² with length 12 cm → breadth = 96 ÷ 12 = 8 cm.

If both length and breadth are doubled, the area becomes 4 times bigger (2 × 2), not 2 times.

Why standard units matter

If everyone measured in hand-spans, no two answers would agree. A standard unit never changes, so a measurement made in India means exactly the same thing anywhere in the world.

Conversions: 1 km = 1000 m · 1 m = 100 cm · 1 kg = 1000 g. So 2.5 km = 2500 m, 150 cm = 1.5 m, 4500 g = 4.5 kg.

The four kinds of motion

Rectilinear — in a straight line (a car on a straight road, a falling stone). Circular — along a circular path (the Earth around the Sun). Rotational — spinning about its own axis (a top, a fan). Periodic — repeating at regular intervals (a pendulum, clock hands).

An object is at rest if its position does not change compared with its surroundings. A light year is not a time — it is the DISTANCE light travels in one year.
