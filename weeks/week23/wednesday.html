🌍 Geography · Ch 6 North America · Part 3
Western Cordilleras, Plateaus & Rain-Shadow Deserts
North America has four physical divisions. This week: the mighty Western Cordilleras, the intermontane plateaus between them, and the deserts created in their rain shadow.

🗺️ Four physical divisions

1. Western Cordilleras (mountain ranges)
2. Canadian Shield
3. Central Plains / Great Plains
4. Eastern Highlands

⛰️ Western Cordilleras

Young fold mountains, high and rugged like the Himalayas. Stretch about 6,500 km from Alaska to Panama, with several ranges running north to south.
• Rocky Mountains — the eastern boundary.
• Alaskan Range (north), Cascade Range (centre), Coastal Range (south) rise steeply from the west coast.
• Sierra Nevada and Western Sierra Madre lie inland, parallel to the Coastal Ranges.

🌋 Molten rock & Yosemite

Over 100 million years ago, while the Rockies underwent violent folding, molten material was thrown up. When it solidified it formed the Columbia Plateau and the gigantic rocky cliffs of the Sierra Nevada, seen in the Yosemite Valley.

🏜️ Intermontane plateaus

An intermontane plateau is a plateau surrounded by mountains. North America's: Columbia Plateau, Great Basin, Colorado Plateau, Mexican Plateau (north to south).

🏔️ High peaks

Many peaks exceed 4,000 m. Highest: Mt McKinley (Denali), Alaska, about 6,190 m. Others: Logan 5,959 m (Canada), Pico de Orizaba 5,610 m (Mexico), Mt St Elias 5,489 m (Alaska), Popocatépetl 5,465 m (active volcano, Mexico).
Active volcanoes in Mexico and Alaska; the region is earthquake-prone.

☔ Rain-shadow deserts

The Cordilleras block moist Pacific winds, creating rain-shadow conditions. Four deserts formed in the south-west: Great Basin, Mojave, Sonoran, Chihuahuan.

💡 Rain-shadow area = little rain because hills shelter it from rain-bearing winds.

🔥 Desert facts

The Great Basin is a cold desert; the Mojave contains Death Valley, one of the hottest places on Earth and the lowest point in North America. The Sonoran and Chihuahuan are hot deserts shared with Mexico.

🎯 Must-remember facts
• Young fold mts · ~6,500 km Alaska→Panama · Rockies = eastern edge
• Alaskan (N), Cascade (centre), Coastal (S) ranges
• Intermontane: Columbia, Great Basin, Colorado, Mexican
• Denali 6,190 m · Logan 5,959 · Orizaba 5,610 · St Elias 5,489 · Popocatépetl 5,465
• Deserts: Great Basin, Mojave, Sonoran, Chihuahuan (rain shadow)
