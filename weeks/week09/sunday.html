🔬 Science & Space

🌑 Total solar eclipse — 12 Aug

The Moon completely covered the Sun along a narrow path across Siberia (Russia), eastern Greenland, western Iceland, northern Spain and a tiny part of Portugal. It was the first total solar eclipse on mainland Europe since 1999. The longest totality was 2 minutes 18 seconds, just off Iceland's west coast. It was not visible from India.

💡 Remember: a solar eclipse happens when the Moon comes between the Sun and the Earth — only on a new moon day.

🏃 Climbing stairs is good for your heart — 13 Aug

A big study that combined data from more than 4,80,000 people found that people who regularly climb stairs had a 39% lower risk of dying from heart disease and a 24% lower risk of death from any cause.

💡 A study that combines many earlier studies is called a meta-analysis.

☄️ In the sky: Perseid meteor shower

Every year around 12–13 August the Perseids peak. The 'shooting stars' are tiny bits of dust left by Comet Swift–Tuttle, burning up as they hit Earth's atmosphere.

🎨 Arts & Culture

🎭 Sangeet Natak Akademi Awards — 13 Aug

President Droupadi Murmu presented the Sangeet Natak Akademi Fellowships and Awards for 2024 and 2025 at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi. About 115 artists were honoured in Hindustani and Carnatic music, Bharatanatyam, Kathak, theatre, puppetry and folk arts. The Fellowship (Akademi Ratna) is the highest honour — seven artists received it, including Rita Ganguly and Sudharani Raghupathy.

💡 Sangeet Natak Akademi is India's national academy for music, dance and drama.

🇮🇳 80th Independence Day — 15 Aug

PM Narendra Modi led the celebrations from the Red Fort, Delhi. The day also marked 150 years of the national song 'Vande Mataram', and celebrated Yuva Shakti (youth power) on the road to a Viksit Bharat by 2047.

💡 'Vande Mataram' was written by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay. The national anthem, 'Jana Gana Mana', was written by Rabindranath Tagore.
🏅 Sports

🏃 European Athletics Championships — 10–16 Aug

Held at the Alexander Stadium, Birmingham (UK) — the first time a British city hosted these championships. Great Britain's Georgia Hunter Bell won the women's 1500 m.

🏑 Women's Hockey World Cup begins — 15 Aug

The FIH Women's Hockey World Cup started, co-hosted by Belgium and the Netherlands, with 16 teams. India's captain is Salima Tete.

🏏 India vs Sri Lanka Test series starts — 15 Aug

India, captained by Shubman Gill, began a 2-Test series in Sri Lanka with the first Test at Galle. The series is part of the ICC World Test Championship.

🏓 Table tennis federation suspended — 12 Aug

The Sports Ministry suspended the recognition of the Table Tennis Federation of India (TTFI) as the national sports federation for table tennis.
