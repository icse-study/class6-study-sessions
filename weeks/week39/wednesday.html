📖 Story time
Darwin and the finches of Galapagos
In 1835, a young naturalist named Charles Darwin landed on the Galapagos Islands off Ecuador aboard HMS Beagle. He noticed that finches on different islands had different beaks — thick for cracking seeds, thin for catching insects. Giant tortoises, too, differed from island to island. These clues from isolated islands helped him form his famous theory of how living things change over time.
