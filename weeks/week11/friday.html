The Digestive System — the food's journey begins 🍽️
Food is our fuel and our building material. But a chapati is far too big to get into your blood! Digestion breaks it into tiny, soluble pieces. This week: mouth → oesophagus → stomach.

Big and small

Macronutrients are needed in large amounts (carbohydrates, proteins, fats). Micronutrients are needed in tiny amounts (vitamins, minerals).

3️⃣ The alimentary canal

One long tube

A continuous muscular tube, about 9 metres long in an adult: mouth → pharynx → oesophagus → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → anus. Glands along the way (salivary glands, liver, pancreas) pour in digestive juices.

Two sets of teeth

Milk teeth: 20, from about 6 months; fall out from about age 6. Permanent teeth: 32 (16 in each jaw). Children have no premolars. The last molars, wisdom teeth, appear around 17–25 years.

💡 One side of one jaw: 2 incisors, 1 canine, 2 premolars, 3 molars = 8. × 4 quarters = 32.

Inside a tooth

Crown (above the gum) · neck (at the gum line) · root (in the jaw bone). Layers: enamel — the hardest substance in the body; dentine — bone-like; pulp cavity — nerves and blood vessels. Cement fixes the root in the jaw.

Tooth decay

Bacteria + sugar stuck on teeth form a sticky film, plaque. The bacteria make acid that dissolves enamel → holes (dental caries). Brush twice a day, especially at night — saliva flow drops while you sleep, so bacteria multiply.

5️⃣ Tongue and saliva

👅 The tongue

A muscular organ that tastes (taste buds for sweet, salty, sour, bitter), mixes food with saliva, rolls it into a ball and pushes it down when you swallow. It also helps us speak.

💧 Saliva

Three pairs of salivary glands make about 1–1.5 litres a day. Saliva moistens food into a soft ball, the bolus; its enzyme salivary amylase (ptyalin) turns starch → maltose (a sugar); it also helps keep the mouth clean.

💡 Digestion of starch starts in the mouth.
6️⃣ Oesophagus (food pipe)

A squeezing tube

About 25 cm long. No digestion happens here. Its walls contract and relax in waves — peristalsis — that push the bolus down, even if you're upside down!

7️⃣ Stomach

The mixer

A J-shaped muscular bag on the left side. Its walls churn food for about 3–4 hours with gastric juice, turning it into a thick soup, chyme.

In gastric juiceJob
Hydrochloric acid (HCl)kills germs; makes the stomach acidic so pepsin can work

Mucuscoats the stomach wall so the acid doesn't digest the stomach itself
💡 Protein digestion starts in the stomach.

👀 Show answer

3. Bolus or chyme — which forms in the stomach?
Soup!
👀 Show answer

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🦷 Enamel is harder than bone — yet it can't repair itself, so a cavity never heals on its own.
• 🧪 Stomach acid is strong enough to dissolve metal, but mucus protects the stomach lining, and the lining renews itself every few days.
• 🤸 Astronauts can swallow in space and you can swallow while doing a headstand — peristalsis, not gravity, moves food.
• 🦈 Sharks can grow and lose thousands of teeth in a lifetime; humans get only two sets.

🗣️ Friday · Week 11 · ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ
ಸಂಯುಕ್ತಾಕ್ಷರ — double-decker letters 🚌
When two or more consonants come together with one vowel, they make a ಸಂಯುಕ್ತಾಕ್ಷರ (also called ಒತ್ತಕ್ಷರ). The second consonant is written small underneath — the ಒತ್ತು.

Formula

ವ್ಯಂಜನ + ವ್ಯಂಜನ + ಸ್ವರ = ಸಂಯುಕ್ತಾಕ್ಷರ · e.g. ಕ್ + ಕ್ + ಅ = ಕ್ಕ (in ಅಕ್ಕ)

TypeMeaningExamples
ಸಜಾತೀಯthe SAME consonant twiceಅಮ್ಮ (ಮ್+ಮ್) · ಅಕ್ಕ · ಅಜ್ಜ · ಅಪ್ಪ · ಹಲ್ಲು
ವಿಜಾತೀಯDIFFERENT consonantsಪುಸ್ತಕ (ಸ್+ತ್) · ಶಕ್ತಿ (ಕ್+ತ್) · ಅಕ್ಷರ (ಕ್+ಷ್) · ಶಸ್ತ್ರ (ಸ್+ತ್+ರ್)
🔴 The two ways to write ರ್ in a cluster
NameWhenExamples
ಅರ್ಕಾವೊತ್ತುರ್ is said FIRSTಅರ್ಧ · ಸೂರ್ಯ · ಕಾರ್ಯ · ಧರ್ಮ · ಪರ್ವತ
ರ-ಒತ್ತುರ್ is said LASTವಕ್ರ · ಅಗ್ರ · ಪ್ರಗತಿ · ಕ್ರಮ · ಚಂದ್ರ

Tip

Say the word slowly: in ಸೂರ್ಯ you hear ರ್ before ಯ → ಅರ್ಕಾವೊತ್ತು. In ಪ್ರಗತಿ you hear ಪ್ before ರ → ರ-ಒತ್ತು.

💡 Some consonants change shape when written as an ಒತ್ತು — e.g. ತ, ನ, ಮ, ಯ, ರ, ಲ.

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🔤 ಕ್ಷ (ಕ್+ಷ್) is so common that many alphabet charts show it as a letter of its own.
• 🧩 ಶಸ್ತ್ರ has THREE consonants (ಸ್+ತ್+ರ್) joined to one vowel.
• ✍️ ಅರ್ಕಾವೊತ್ತು is written after the letter, even though ರ್ is spoken first!
