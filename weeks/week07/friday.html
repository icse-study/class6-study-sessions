🧬 Diagram Recall — The Digestive System

The path of food, in order

Mouth (chewing; saliva contains the enzyme amylase, which breaks down starch) → Oesophagus (the food pipe) → Stomach (churning; gastric juice) → Small intestine (absorption of nutrients through finger-like villi) → Large intestine (absorption of water; faeces formed) → Anus (elimination).

Two helper organs sit alongside: the liver produces bile (stored in the gall bladder) and the pancreas produces digestive enzymes. The small intestine is the longest part of the whole system.
🗣️ ಕನ್ನಡ — ಸಂಧಿ

ಸಂಧಿ ಎಂದರೇನು?

ಸಂಧಿ is what happens when two sounds meet at the junction of two words (or parts of words) and merge into a single sound. Its purpose is simply to make pronunciation smoother and easier.

ವಿಧDeals with
ಸ್ವರ ಸಂಧಿ (Swara)Vowels meeting vowels
ವ್ಯಂಜನ ಸಂಧಿ (Vyanjana)Consonants
ವಿಸರ್ಗ ಸಂಧಿ (Visarga)The visarga sound

ಉದಾಹರಣೆಗಳು — examples to know

ದೇವ + ಆಲಯ = ದೇವಾಲಯ (temple) · ವಿದ್ಯಾ + ಅರ್ಥಿ = ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿ (student) · ಸೂರ್ಯ + ಉದಯ = ಸೂರ್ಯೋದಯ (sunrise) · ಗಂಗಾ + ಅಂಬರ = ಗಂಗಾಂಬರ. All four are ಸ್ವರ ಸಂಧಿ, because a vowel meets a vowel each time.

Careful: ಸಮಾಸ (compound words) is a different topic altogether — it is not a type of ಸಂಧಿ.

ಸಹಾಯ — how to structure it

An essay needs three parts. ಪ್ರಸ್ತಾವನೆ (introduction): when it was held and who was the chief guest. ವಿಷಯ (body): two or three paragraphs on the events — ನೃತ್ಯ, ಹಾಡು, ನಾಟಕ, ಬಹುಮಾನ ವಿತರಣೆ. ಉಪಸಂಹಾರ (conclusion): what you enjoyed most and what you learnt. Ten to twelve sentences.

🧬 Biology — The Skeletal System

Bones and what they protect

Skull → the brain · Ribcage (12 pairs of ribs) → the heart and lungs · Backbone → the spinal cord · Pelvis → the lower organs, and carries the body's weight.

The femur (thigh bone) is the longest and strongest bone. The upper arm is the humerus; the forearm has the radius and ulna; the lower leg has the tibia and fibula. An adult has about 206 bones.

Four functions

Support · Protection · Movement (with the muscles) · Production of blood cells in the bone marrow. The skeleton is made of bones and cartilage.
