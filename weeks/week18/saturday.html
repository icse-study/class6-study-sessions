Diet detective 🔍🥗
A doctor can often guess what's missing from a diet just by looking at the patient. Learn the balanced plate, solve the cases, then play the card game with your family.

🍽️ The balanced plate — three food groups

A balanced diet has food from all three groups, plus water and fibre (roughage).
🕵️ Diet detective — solve the case
Case 1. Riya can't see well when the lights go dim in the evening.
Eyes at night.
👀 Show answer
Lack of vitamin A — night blindness. Eat carrots, yellow fruits, milk.
Case 2. A little boy has bowed legs and soft bones; he rarely goes outdoors.
Sunshine vitamin.
👀 Show answer
Rickets — lack of vitamin D. Morning sunlight, eggs, fish liver oil, milk.
Case 3. A woman feels tired all the time and looks pale.
Blood's red pigment.
👀 Show answer
Anaemia — lack of iron. Green leafy vegetables, bananas, cereals, egg yolk.
Case 4. A man in a hilly area far from the sea has a large swelling in his neck.
Sea food and salt.
👀 Show answer
Goitre — lack of iodine. Use iodised salt.
Case 5. A child has a swollen belly and puffy legs, and eats only rice and a little vegetable.
No dal, no eggs, no milk.
👀 Show answer
Kwashiorkor — lack of protein.
Case 6. After a long hot day of cricket, a player gets painful muscle cramps.
Lost in sweat.
👀 Show answer
Loss of sodium and potassium — drink water with a little salt and sugar, eat fruit.
🃏 The matching card game (from your textbook)

How to play

Make 18 cards: 6 diseases, 6 nutrients, 6 food sources. Shuffle, deal 3 each, and take turns picking up and throwing away one card. The first to hold a matching set of 3 — disease + nutrient + source — wins!

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🥕 Carrots really do help your eyes — they're full of beta-carotene, which the body turns into vitamin A.
• 🧂 India made iodised salt compulsory for eating purposes to fight goitre.
• 🌱 Sprouting seeds such as moong suddenly gain lots of vitamin C — a cheap way to prevent scurvy.
