The Respiratory System — every breath you take 🌬️
You can live weeks without food, days without water, but only minutes without oxygen. Today: how air gets in, how oxygen reaches your blood, and what your cells do with it.

1️⃣ Breathing is not respiration!
BreathingRespiration
whattaking air in and out of the lungsbreaking down glucose to release energy
typephysical processchemical (biochemical) process
wherelungs and air passagesinside every cell
energyuses energyreleases energy (as ATP)

The respiration equation

Glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + energy (ATP)

💡 It is photosynthesis run backwards! Aerobic respiration uses oxygen. Anaerobic respiration happens without oxygen — in tired muscles it makes lactic acid (that's the ache), and in yeast it makes alcohol and CO₂.

Why breathe through the nose, not the mouth?

The mouth has no hairs to filter dust and doesn't warm the air as well. Nose-breathing sends cleaner, warmer, moister air to the lungs.

3️⃣ The lungs

Two spongy bags

The lungs sit in the chest (thoracic) cavity, protected by the ribcage. Each is wrapped in a double membrane, the pleura, with fluid between the layers that stops rubbing. The right lung has 3 lobes; the left lung has 2 — it is smaller to make room for the heart. Below the lungs lies a dome-shaped muscle sheet, the diaphragm.

💡 Put your hands on your ribs and breathe in deeply — feel them lift and spread. That's inhalation.
5️⃣ Gas exchange in the alveoli

The swap

Each alveolus is wrapped in tiny blood capillaries. Oxygen passes from the air into the blood, where it joins haemoglobin in the red blood cells. Carbon dioxide passes from the blood into the alveolus to be breathed out.

Why alveoli are perfect: walls one cell thick · always moist · a rich blood supply · hundreds of millions of them, giving a huge surface area

🚭 Smoking

Smoke paralyses the cilia, coats the lungs with tar and damages the alveoli. It is a major cause of bronchitis and lung cancer — and passive smoking (breathing others' smoke) harms too.

🎲 Fun Corner
1. Why do you cough if you laugh while eating?
A lid failed to close.
👀 Show answer
The epiglottis didn't close in time, and food touched the windpipe — coughing throws it out.
2. Which lung is smaller, and why?
Neighbour.
👀 Show answer
The left — it makes room for the heart.
3. Breathe on a cold mirror. Why does it mist up?
Exhaled air.
👀 Show answer
Exhaled air carries water vapour, which condenses on the cold glass.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🎻 Your vocal cords vibrate like violin strings. Tighter cords give a higher voice — that's why a boy's voice "breaks" when his larynx grows.
• 🛡️ The C-shaped rings keep your windpipe open like the rings of a vacuum-cleaner hose; the open side lets the food pipe behind it expand when you swallow.
• 🐬 Dolphins and whales breathe air through a blowhole on top of their head.
• 🤧 Your eyes almost always shut when you sneeze — it is part of an automatic reflex.
