🥷 Hiding and fooling

Camouflage

Blending with surroundings: the stick insect, the twig-like caterpillar, the chameleon, the snow-white arctic hare in winter.

Warning colours are the opposite — bright wasps and poison frogs say "don't eat me!"
✈️ Long journeys — migration

Winter visitors

Birds such as Siberian cranes, flamingos and bar-headed geese fly thousands of kilometres to Indian lakes like Keoladeo (Bharatpur) to escape freezing winters.

💡 Bar-headed geese fly over the Himalayas — breathing the thinnest air any bird is known to fly in.
