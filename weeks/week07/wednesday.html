Why we get seasons

The Earth's axis — the imaginary line through the North and South poles — is tilted at 23.5°. Because of that tilt, as the Earth revolves, different parts lean towards the Sun at different times of the year and so receive different amounts of sunlight.

Seasons need BOTH revolution AND the tilt. Distance from the Sun is not the cause — a favourite exam trap.

✍️ English — Modals

What a modal does

A modal is a helping verb that shows the speaker's attitude — ability, permission, obligation, possibility or advice. It is always followed by the base form of the main verb: "She can swim", never "can swims".

ModalUsed forExample
canAbility, informal permissionI can swim.
couldPast ability, polite requestI could swim at five. Could you help?
mayFormal permission, possibilityMay I come in?
mightPossibility, less certainIt might rain.
mustObligation, strong prohibitionYou must wear a seatbelt.
shouldAdvice, recommendationYou should study.
willFuture, promise, willingnessI will call you.
wouldPolite request, hypotheticalI would travel if I were rich.

The pairs people mix up

may vs might — both show possibility, but might is less certain. must vs should — must is an obligation you cannot refuse, should is only advice. can vs may for permission — may is the more formal.
