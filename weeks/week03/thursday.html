Watch the traps

For a triangle the height must be perpendicular to the base — not a slanting side. For a circle, 2πr is the distance around it (circumference) while πr² is the space inside it (area).

💡 π ≈ 22/7 ≈ 3.14. Area formulas always give square units (cm²); perimeter gives plain units (cm).
🧊 Volume & surface area (3-D)

Why 6a² ?

A cube has 6 identical square faces, and each face has area a². So the total surface area is 6 × a² = 6a². Volume uses all three dimensions, so it is a × a × a = a³ and is measured in cubic units (cm³).

📊 Statistics, algebra & temperature

Mean

Mean = Sum of all observations ÷ Number of observations

Marks 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 → sum 40 ÷ 5 = mean 8.

Algebraic identities

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
(a + b)(a − b) = a² − b²

💡 The commonest mistake is writing (a + b)² = a² + b². The middle term 2ab is never optional.

Celsius → Fahrenheit

F = (9/5) × C + 32  and back: C = (5/9) × (F − 32)

C = 100 → F = (9/5)(100) + 32 = 180 + 32 = 212 °F (boiling point of water).

🧱 Matter and its states

Matter

Matter is anything that occupies space and has mass. It is made of tiny particles that are always moving and that attract one another.

Air, water, sugar, iron, wood, milk — all matter. Light, heat and sound are NOT matter (no mass, no volume).
Property🧊 Solid💧 Liquid💨 Gas

VolumeDefiniteDefiniteNot definite
Particle spacingVery closeSlightly apartVery far apart

Why gases compress so easily

Gas particles are very far apart with large empty spaces between them and almost no force of attraction. Pressing them simply pushes the particles closer into that empty space. In solids and liquids the particles are already touching, so there is nowhere left to go.

💡 That is why a cylinder can hold a whole roomful of LPG.

Boiling vs evaporation

Boiling happens at one fixed temperature, throughout the whole liquid, and needs heating. Evaporation happens at any temperature, only at the surface, and is a cooling process.

🧪 Pure substances & mixtures

The two families

A pure substance has only one kind of particle. The two types are elements (one kind of atom — iron, oxygen, gold) and compounds (two or more elements chemically joined in a fixed ratio — water, salt, CO₂).

A mixture has two or more substances simply mixed, in any ratio, with no chemical bonding — so its parts keep their own properties and can be separated by physical means.

Air is a mixture — nitrogen, oxygen, carbon dioxide and water vapour are not chemically combined, their proportions vary from place to place, and they can be separated physically.

🔬 Symbols & the lab

Useful facts

Chemical symbol of water = H₂O (2 hydrogen atoms + 1 oxygen atom). Carbon dioxide = CO₂, common salt = NaCl, oxygen = O₂.

Temperature is measured with a thermometer (a laboratory thermometer reads about −10 °C to 110 °C). Mass is measured with a beam balance, and volume of a liquid with a measuring cylinder.

💡 On the Celsius scale water freezes at 0 °C and boils at 100 °C.

📦 Articles & Negation

Definite articles (the)

le — masculine singular (le livre) · la — feminine singular (la table) · l' — before a vowel or silent h (l'école, l'homme) · les — plural (les livres).

Making it negative: ne … pas

Put ne before the verb and pas after it. Before a vowel, ne → n'.

Je ne parle pas français. · Nous n'aimons pas la grammaire.
