✍️ Writing Practice — answer on paper first (not scored)
1. Compare the honesty of Ramchand’s father with the grown-ups in “The Little Prince”.
👀 Show model answer
Ramchand’s father honestly admits, “But I don’t know that either”, which makes his son feel understood. The grown-ups in “The Little Prince” pretend to know everything and dismiss the boy’s drawing as a hat, never admitting they cannot see what he sees. Honest grown-ups build trust; proud ones make children lonely.
