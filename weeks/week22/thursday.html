⚗️ Chemistry · Week 22 · Ch 5 Pure Substances & Mixtures (Part 2)
Separating mixtures — pick the right method for the right property
We separate mixtures to get useful substances, remove harmful ones and obtain pure substances. The method depends on the type of mixture and on a property of its parts: size, weight, magnetism, solubility or boiling point.

✋ Solid + solid (1)

• Hand-picking: small quantity, big or coloured impurities — stones from rice, pulses, spices
• Winnowing: wind blows away lighter husk while heavy grain falls straight down
• Sieving: particles of different sizes; fine sieves for flour, big ones for sand and gravel; jewellers sort pearls; cashew grading

🧲 Solid + solid (2)

• Magnetic separation: one part is magnetic — iron from sulphur or sand
• Sublimation: one part sublimes — ammonium chloride or iodine from salt or sand. Heat in a dish under an inverted funnel with a cotton plug; vapour solidifies on the funnel's cool neck.
• Solvent extraction: one part dissolves — sand + salt: add water, salt dissolves, sand stays; evaporate to get salt back.

💡 Sublimable: camphor, naphthalene, iodine, ammonium chloride, dry ice.

⏳ Solid + liquid (insoluble)

• Sedimentation: heavy insoluble solid settles → sediment; clear liquid above = supernatant
• Decantation: gently pouring off the clear liquid without disturbing the sediment
• Loading (coagulation): adding alum makes very fine clay particles clump and settle faster
• Filtration: liquid passes through a filter → filtrate; solid left behind → residue

💡 Filters: filter paper, sand, charcoal, cotton, glass wool, muslin, unglazed porcelain, tea strainer.

📄 Folding a filter paper

Fold a circle in half, then in half again, and open it into a cone with three layers on one side and one on the other. Fit it about a quarter-inch below the funnel's rim and pour the mixture down a glass rod.

♨️ Solid + liquid (soluble)

• Evaporation: heat the solution until the liquid escapes; only the solid is recovered. Salt from sea water in shallow pans under the sun.
• Distillation: evaporate and then condense the vapour — you get both the pure liquid (distillate) and the solid. Tap water → distilled water.
• Gas + liquid: heat it — boiled water tastes flat because the dissolved air escapes.

📋 Method ↔ principle

hand-picking: big, few · winnowing: light vs heavy · sieving: size · magnet: magnetic · sublimation: sublimes · solvent extraction: one dissolves · sedimentation/decantation: heavy solid · loading: very fine particles · filtration: insoluble, complete separation · evaporation: soluble solid · distillation: pure liquid needed · separating funnel: immiscible liquids

✨ Fun facts
• Crude petroleum is separated in tall towers into LPG, petrol, kerosene and diesel because each boils at a different temperature.
• Gujarat's Little Rann of Kutch produces a huge share of India's salt by evaporating sea water in the sun.
• Your kidneys are filters: they clean about 180 litres of blood fluid every day.
• Cream is separated from milk in dairies by spinning it very fast — a method called centrifugation.

🎯 Must-remember
• Hand-picking, winnowing, sieving, magnet, sublimation, solvent extraction: solid+solid
• Sedimentation → decantation; alum = loading
• Filtration: residue stays, filtrate passes
• Evaporation: solid only · Distillation: both
• Separating funnel: immiscible liquids
