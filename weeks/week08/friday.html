🧬 Diagram Recall — The Respiratory System

The path of air — learn it in order

Nostrils → nasal cavity → pharynx → larynx → trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli

Every label in a diagram question comes from this chain. Say it aloud three times, then draw it from memory.
PartWhat it does
Nose / nostrilsAir enters; hairs and mucus filter dust and germs, and the air is warmed and moistened
LarynxThe voice box — produces sound
EpiglottisA flap that covers the windpipe while you swallow, so food does not enter it
Trachea (windpipe)Carries air down; held permanently open by C-shaped rings of cartilage
BronchiTwo tubes, one entering each lung
BronchiolesThe finer branches inside the lungs
AlveoliTiny air sacs — the site of gas exchange
LungsThe main organs; right lung has 3 lobes, left lung has 2
DiaphragmThe sheet of muscle below the lungs that drives breathing

The mechanism of breathing

Inhalation: the diaphragm contracts and flattens downward, ribs move up and out, chest volume increases, air rushes in.
Exhalation: the diaphragm relaxes and domes upward, chest volume decreases, air is pushed out.

💡 Down = in, up = out. If you can remember which way the diaphragm moves, you can never get this question wrong.
🗣️ ಕನ್ನಡ — ಕಾಲಗಳು (Tenses) & ಪತ್ರ ಲೇಖನ

ಮೂರು ಕಾಲಗಳು · The three tenses

ವರ್ತಮಾನ ಕಾಲ (present) — the action is happening now.
ಭೂತ ಕಾಲ (past) — the action is already over.
ಭವಿಷ್ಯತ್ ಕಾಲ (future) — the action is yet to happen.

The verb ending changes with both the tense and the subject — this is called ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದ ರೂಪ (conjugation).
ಕ್ರಿಯಾಪದವರ್ತಮಾನಭೂತಭವಿಷ್ಯತ್
ಓದು (read) · ನಾನುಓದುತ್ತೇನೆಓದಿದೆನುಓದುವೆನು
ಹೋಗು (go) · ನಾನುಹೋಗುತ್ತೇನೆಹೋದೆನುಹೋಗುವೆನು
ಮಾಡು (do) · ನಾನುಮಾಡುತ್ತೇನೆಮಾಡಿದೆನುಮಾಡುವೆನು
ಹೇಳು (say) · ನಾವುಹೇಳುತ್ತೇವೆಹೇಳಿದೆವುಹೇಳುವೆವು
ಬರು (come) · ಅವಳುಬರುತ್ತಾಳೆಬಂದಳುಬರುವಳು

ಗುರುತಿಸುವ ಸೂತ್ರ · The spotting rule

Look at the ending: -ಉತ್ತೇನೆ / -ಉತ್ತಾನೆ / -ಉತ್ತಾಳೆ → present · -ಇದೆನು / -ಇದನು / -ಇದಳು → past · -ವೆನು / -ವನು / -ವಳು → future.

ಸ್ಥಳ ಮತ್ತು ದಿನಾಂಕ (place and date) → ಸಂಬೋಧನೆ (salutation) → ವಿಷಯ (body) → ಮುಕ್ತಾಯ (closing) → ಹೆಸರು (name).

🧬 Biology — Respiration in Detail

Breathing is not the same as respiration

Breathing (ventilation) is the physical act of taking air in and pushing it out.
Respiration is the chemical process inside every cell that releases energy from food using oxygen.

glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy

Gas exchange in the alveoli

Alveoli are extremely thin-walled and wrapped in blood capillaries. Oxygen diffuses from the air in the alveoli into the blood; carbon dioxide diffuses from the blood into the alveoli to be breathed out. The process is diffusion — movement from high concentration to low.

💡 There are around 300 million alveoli in a pair of lungs. Spread flat they would cover a badminton court — that is why the exchange is so fast.

Defences and control

Mucus traps dust and germs; tiny hair-like cilia sweep the mucus upward and out. Breathing rate is controlled automatically by the medulla oblongata in the brain — which is why you keep breathing while asleep.

Common respiratory disorders

Common cold — viral infection of the nasal passage. Asthma — the airways become inflamed and narrow, usually from an allergy. Bronchitis — inflammation of the bronchi. Pneumonia — infection that fills the alveoli with fluid.

💡 Smoking destroys the cilia and the alveolar walls. Anaemia is a blood disorder, not a respiratory one — a favourite trap in exams.
