➗ Maths — Introduction to Algebra

Three words to get right

A variable is a letter standing for an unknown number (x, y, a). An algebraic expression combines variables, numbers and operators but has no equals sign (2x + 3). An equation says two expressions are equal — it does have an equals sign (2x + 3 = 11).

Solving an equation — keep the balance

Whatever you do to one side you must do to the other. Add, subtract, multiply or divide both sides by the same number until the variable stands alone.

2x + 3 = 11 → subtract 3 from both sides: 2x = 8 → divide both sides by 2: x = 4. Check: 2(4) + 3 = 11 ✔

Formulas still needed

Perimeter of a square = 4 × side · Area of a square = side² · Perimeter of a rectangle = 2(l + b) · Area of a rectangle = l × b.

Changes of state

Heat added: melting (solid→liquid), evaporation and boiling (liquid→gas). Heat removed: condensation (gas→liquid), freezing (liquid→solid).

Heating makes particles move faster and spread apart; cooling slows them and draws them closer. Matter is anything with mass that occupies space — energy is not matter.
🇫🇷 French — Regular ‑er Verbs

How to conjugate

Take the infinitive, remove ‑er to get the stem (parler → parl‑), then add the ending for the subject.

SubjectEndingparler
je‑eje parle
tu‑estu parles
il / elle / on‑eil parle
nous‑onsnous parlons
vous‑ezvous parlez
ils / elles‑entils parlent

Verbs & a pronunciation tip

parler (to speak) · aimer (to like/love) · manger (to eat) · chanter (to sing) · danser (to dance) · regarder (to watch) · écouter (to listen).

The endings ‑e, ‑es and ‑ent are all silent, so je parle, tu parles and ils parlent sound identical. Only the spelling tells them apart — which is why dictation matters.
