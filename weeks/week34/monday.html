🔁 Quick recap

1816 Mohr writes “Stille Nacht” → 23 Dec 1818 broken organ, actors perform in a home, Mohr walks to a hilltop → 24 Dec Gruber writes a guitar tune; Oberndorf congregation hears it → Mauracher repairs the organ, is spellbound, takes copies to Kapfing → Rainers (USA) and Strassers (northern Europe) → English translation 1863 → UNESCO 2011 → Mohr’s manuscript found 1995.

✍️ Writing Practice — answer on paper first (not scored)
1. Do you think “Silent Night” would take as long to become popular if it were written today?
👀 Show model answer
No. In 1818 the carol spread slowly, carried by hand-written copies and travelling singers, and took 45 years to be translated into English. Today it could be recorded, shared online and translated within days, reaching millions of people around the world almost instantly.
