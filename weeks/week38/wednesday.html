📖 Story time
Ibn Battuta, the boy who walked the world
In 1325, a 21-year-old from Morocco named Ibn Battuta set out for Mecca — and kept going for 29 years, covering about 1,20,000 km across Africa, Arabia, Central Asia, India, China and the Maldives. He served as a judge in Delhi under Muhammad bin Tughlaq. His book, the Rihla, describes deserts, monsoon seas, rice fields and mountain passes — a geography textbook written by a traveller!
