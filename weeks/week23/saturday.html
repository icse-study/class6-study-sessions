💧 Diarrhoea and ORS

Losing water fast

Diarrhoea (loose motions) drains water and salts, causing dehydration. ORS (oral rehydration solution) replaces them.

Home ORS: 1 litre of clean boiled water + 6 level teaspoons of sugar + ½ teaspoon of salt
💡 ORS has saved millions of children's lives and costs only a few rupees.

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🦷 Sugary drinks sipped slowly all day harm teeth more than a single sweet eaten at once — the acid attack keeps going.
• 🧪 ORS is sometimes called one of the most important medical advances of the 20th century.
• 🪱 A tapeworm can grow several metres long inside the human intestine.
