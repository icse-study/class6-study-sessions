Flowers and their helpers 🐝🦋🐦
A flower cannot walk to meet another flower. It uses wind, water or animals as messengers — and its shape, colour and smell show exactly which messenger it wants.
1️⃣ Bisexual or unisexual?
Bisexual flowerUnisexual flower
has both stamens and carpelshas only stamens (male) or only carpels (female)

Complete and incomplete

A complete flower has all four whorls (calyx, corolla, androecium, gynoecium). If any whorl is missing, it is incomplete.

💡 Every unisexual flower is incomplete!

Self-pollination

In pea, pollen falls on the stigma of the same flower before it even opens. No messenger needed — but less variety in the offspring.

3️⃣ Why pollinators matter

Food depends on them

Many fruits, vegetables and nuts need pollinators. Farmers keep bee boxes near fields of mustard and sunflower to get more seeds.

💡 Bees see ultraviolet light, so many flowers have hidden UV patterns that point to the nectar.

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🐝 A honeybee visits about 50–100 flowers on a single trip out of the hive.
• 🍫 Chocolate depends on tiny flies called midges, which pollinate cacao flowers.
• 🦇 Some bats have tongues longer than their own bodies for reaching nectar.
