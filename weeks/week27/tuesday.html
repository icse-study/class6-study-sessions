Turtle — Shapes, Colours & Loops
Add colour, fill and circles, and use for loops to draw stars, polygons and spirals.
🎨 Colour and fill
t.color("red")              # pen colour
t.color("red", "yellow")    # pen red, fill yellow
t.pensize(5)                # thicker lines
turtle.bgcolor("black")     # background

t.begin_fill()
for i in range(4):
    t.forward(80)
    t.right(90)
t.end_fill()                # fills the square

The fill appears only when end_fill() runs.

⭕ Circles
t.circle(50)        # full circle, radius 50
t.circle(50, 180)   # half circle
t.dot(20)           # a filled dot
t.hideturtle()      # hide the arrow

The centre of the circle is 50 steps to the turtle’s left.

⭐ Stars and polygons
# 5-pointed star: turn 144 each time
for i in range(5):
    t.forward(150)
    t.right(144)

def polygon(n, size):
    for i in range(n):
        t.forward(size)
        t.right(360 / n)

polygon(8, 40)      # octagon
🌈 Rainbow spiral challenge
colors = ["red", "orange", "yellow", "green", "blue", "purple"]
for i in range(60):
    t.color(colors[i % 6])
    t.forward(i * 5)
    t.right(90)

The lines get longer each time, so it spirals outwards. i % 6 cycles through the 6 colours.

✨ Fun facts
• A 5-pointed star turns 144° five times = 720°, so the turtle spins round twice while drawing it.
• A polygon with many sides, like 100, looks almost exactly like a circle.
• Turtle understands hundreds of colour names, such as "gold", "skyblue" and "hotpink".

🎯 Must-remember
• color(pen, fill) · begin_fill … end_fill
• circle(r) · circle(r, angle) · dot(size)
• polygon turn = 360 / n · star turn = 144
• colors[i % len(colors)] cycles colours
