🪜 Levels of organisation

From cell to organism

cell → tissue (similar cells) → organ (tissues working together) → organ system → organism

Example: cardiac muscle cell → muscle tissue → heart → circulatory system → you

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🧍 Your body makes about 2 million new red blood cells every second.
• 🦠 A single white blood cell can swallow many bacteria before it dies.
• 🌱 One rye plant was found to have about 14 billion root hairs!
