🛡️ How blood fights back

WBCs on patrol

Some WBCs swallow germs; others make antibodies that stick to germs and destroy them. After an infection, the body "remembers" the germ.

💡 A vaccine trains WBCs with a harmless form of a germ, so they are ready for the real one — e.g. BCG for TB.

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 👑 Haemophilia was called the "royal disease" because it passed through Queen Victoria's descendants in European royal families.
• 🦟 Malaria parasites often burst out of RBCs at the same time — which is why the fever comes back in a regular rhythm.
• 💉 The word "vaccine" comes from Latin vacca, a cow — the first vaccine used cowpox to protect against smallpox.
