The Flower — from bloom to baby plant 🌸➡️🌱
A flower is the reproductive organ of a plant. Follow its journey: flower → pollination → fertilisation → fruit and seed → germination → new plant.

1️⃣ The four whorls (rings) of a flower

Stalk and base

The flower stands on a stalk, the pedicel. Its swollen tip, the thalamus, carries four rings of parts.

Whorl (outside → in)Made ofJob
1. Calyxgreen sepalsprotects the flower in the bud
2. Corollacoloured, scented petalsattracts insects for pollination
3. Androecium ♂stamens = filament + antheranther makes pollen grains (male)
4. Gynoecium ♀ (pistil)carpels = stigma + style + ovaryovary holds ovules (each has an egg cell)

Memory hook

Can Cats Act Good? — Calyx, Corolla, Androecium, Gynoecium.

💡 Andro = man (male part) · Gyno = woman (female part). The calyx and corolla are helpers, not reproductive.

3️⃣ Fertilisation

The meeting

A pollen grain on the stigma grows a pollen tube down the style into the ovule. The male gamete fuses with the egg cell → a zygote, which grows into the embryo.

💡 Pollination = pollen arrives. Fertilisation = male and female cells fuse. Pollination always comes first.

5️⃣ Fruits

Dry and fleshy

Dry fruits have a dry, papery or hard wall — pea pod, mustard, groundnut, sunflower "seed". Fleshy fruits have a juicy wall — mango, tomato, grapes, orange.

Layer of pericarpIn a mangoJob
Epicarpthe skinprotects the fruit
Mesocarpthe juicy pulp you eatstores food; tempts animals to spread the seed
Endocarpthe hard stoneguards the seed

False fruit

In an apple or pear, the part you eat grows from the thalamus, not the ovary — so it is called a false fruit. The real ovary is the papery core around the pips.

7️⃣ Germination — the seed wakes up

Three needs

💧 Water — softens the seed coat, wakes up enzymes · 🌬️ Air (oxygen) — for respiration to release energy · 🌡️ Warmth — a suitable temperature. Light is not needed.

🎲 Fun Corner
1. Which part of the flower becomes the fruit?
It holds the ovules.
👀 Show answer
The ovary.
2. Maize flowers are dull and scentless. Why don't they need colour?
Who carries the pollen?
👀 Show answer
They are wind-pollinated, so they needn't attract insects.
3. What is the hard stone of a mango?
Innermost layer.
👀 Show answer
The endocarp, with the seed inside.
🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🍏 Fake apples: the crunchy flesh of an apple is swollen thalamus. The true fruit is the core!
• 🌽 Secret fruit: every maize kernel is a complete one-seeded fruit, because its seed coat is fused to the fruit wall.
• 🚤 Tiny boats: Vallisneria's male flowers break free underwater, float up and sail across the surface until they bump into female flowers.
• 🌸 The biggest single flower in the world, Rafflesia, can be about 1 metre across and smells like rotting meat — to attract flies, its pollinators.

ಗುಣಿತಾಕ್ಷರ — letter maths ➕
A consonant on its own (ಕ್) cannot be said easily. Add a vowel and it becomes a full letter: ವ್ಯಂಜನ + ಸ್ವರ = ಗುಣಿತಾಕ್ಷರ.
SumResult
ಕ್ + ಅಕ
ಸ್ + ಆಸಾ
ಯ್ + ಉಯು
ಮ್ + ಇಮಿ
ನ್ + ಎನೆ
ದ್ + ಓದೋ
🌿 ಕಾಗುಣಿತ — one consonant, 15 forms (a ಬಳ್ಳಿ)

The ಕ-ಬಳ್ಳಿ

ಕ ಕಾ ಕಿ ಕೀ ಕು ಕೂ ಕೃ ಕೆ ಕೇ ಕೈ ಕೊ ಕೋ ಕೌ ಕಂ ಕಃ

💡 A row like this is called a ಬಳ್ಳಿ (creeper) — 13 vowels + 2 yogavahas = 15 forms.

Vowel signs (ಸ್ವರ ಚಿಹ್ನೆ)

Inside a word, a vowel is written as a small sign on the consonant, not as a full letter: ಕ → ಕಾ (ದೀರ್ಘ sign), ಕಿ (ಗುಡಿಸು), ಕು (ಕೊಂಬು), ಕೆ (ಏತ್ವ), ಕೈ (ಐತ್ವ), ಕೌ (ಔತ್ವ). The mark on top of ಕ itself is the ತಲೆಕಟ್ಟು.

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🔢 34 consonants × 15 forms = 510 guṇitākṣaras — that's why Kannada keyboards use vowel-sign keys!
• 🌿 The name ಕಾಗುಣಿತ comes from "ಕ" + "ಗುಣಿತ" — the ಕ-row is the first one children learn.
• ✍️ Kannada is written in syllables: each letter shape usually stands for a whole consonant-plus-vowel sound.
