🗣️ Kannada — quick recall

Words worth ten marks

ಪುಸ್ತಕ (book) · ಮರ (tree) · ಮನೆ (house) · ಸೂರ್ಯ (sun, also ರವಿ / ಭಾಸ್ಕರ) · ಸ್ನೇಹಿತ (friend) · ಶುಭೋದಯ (good morning) · ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು (thank you).

Opposites: ಸುಲಭ (easy) ↔ ಕಠಿಣ / ಕಷ್ಟ (difficult) · ದೊಡ್ಡ ↔ ಸಣ್ಣ. Plurals: ಮರ → ಮರಗಳು, ಹುಡುಗ → ಹುಡುಗರು.
🇫🇷 French — quick recall

Everyday phrases

S'il vous plaît (please) · Bon après-midi (good afternoon) · Merci beaucoup (thank you very much) · À demain (see you tomorrow) · Je m'appelle… (my name is…).

Être: je suis, tu es, il/elle est, nous sommes, vous êtes, ils/elles sont. Counting: un, deux, trois, quatre, cinq … dix (10), quinze (15), vingt (20).

The map half

Equator 0° · Prime Meridian 0° longitude · Tropic of Cancer 23½° N · Tropic of Capricorn 23½° S. Largest ocean: Pacific. Smallest continent: Australia. Physical maps show mountains in brown and water in blue.

The history half
