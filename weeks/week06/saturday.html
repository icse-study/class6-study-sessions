➗ Maths — Ratio, Proportion & the Unitary Method

Ratio

A ratio compares two quantities by division, written a : b. Always simplify it, and always convert to the same unit first.

20 cm to 1 m is NOT 20 : 1. Convert first: 1 m = 100 cm, so the ratio is 20 : 100 = 1 : 5.

Proportion

A proportion says two ratios are equal: a : b = c : d. To test one, cross-multiply — if a × d = b × c, they are in proportion.

3 : 7 = x : 14 → 3 × 14 = 7x → 42 = 7x → x = 6.

Unitary method — and the inverse trap

Direct: more items, more cost. Find the value of ONE first. 8 pencils cost ₹40 → one costs ₹5 → 15 cost ₹75.

Inverse: more workers, LESS time. Multiply to get the total work first. 20 men × 12 days = 240 man-days; to finish in 8 days you need 240 ÷ 8 = 30 men.

Sharing in a ratio

Add the parts, divide the total by that, then multiply out. Two numbers in the ratio 5 : 6 with a sum of 77 → 11 parts → one part = 7 → the numbers are 35 and 42.

Careful

Pyramids belong to Egypt, not the Indus Valley. The Harappans were farmers, traders and artisans, and Lothal was their great dockyard port.

🏛️ Civics — Panchayati Raj

Three tiers, bottom to top

Gram Panchayat (village, headed by the Sarpanch) → Panchayat Samiti (block) → Zilla Parishad (district — the highest tier).

Their work: water, roads, sanitation, street lights, schools and local development. The whole point is to bring decision-making down to the people it affects.
