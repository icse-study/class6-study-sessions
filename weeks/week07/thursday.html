➗ Maths — Rational Numbers

What counts as rational

A rational number can be written as p/q where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0. In standard form, p and q share no common factor except 1, and q is positive.

3/4 is rational. √2 and π are NOT — they cannot be written as one integer over another.
OperationRule
Additiona/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd
Subtractiona/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd
Multiplicationa/b × c/d = ac / bd
Divisiona/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c

Two inverses — do not mix them up

The additive inverse of −7/8 is 7/8 (they add to 0). The reciprocal, or multiplicative inverse, of −5/7 is −7/5 (they multiply to 1).

⚗️ Chemistry — Water

Properties worth memorising

Water is H₂O — two hydrogen atoms to one oxygen. It boils at 100 °C and freezes at 0 °C. It is called the universal solvent because it dissolves more substances than any other liquid.

Water is densest at 4 °C, and ice is less dense than liquid water — which is why ice floats and why ponds freeze from the top down.

The water cycle

Evaporation (liquid → vapour) → condensation (vapour → droplets, forming clouds) → precipitation (rain or snow) → collection. Rain that soaks into the ground is infiltration; rain that flows over the surface is runoff.

Most of the Earth's water is salty ocean. The largest store of fresh water is locked in glaciers and ice caps — which is exactly why conservation and rainwater harvesting matter.
🇫🇷 French — Les Adjectifs

Agreement

A French adjective must agree with its noun in gender and number. Usually add ‑e for feminine and ‑s for plural: grand → grande → grands → grandes.

Irregulars: bon → bonne → bonnes · beau → belle · vieux → vieille · nouveau → nouveaux. An adjective already ending in ‑e (rapide) does not change for the feminine, and one already ending in ‑s (gros) does not change for the plural.

Position — and the BAGS rule

Most French adjectives come after the noun: une maison bleue. But a small group comes before it — remember BAGS: Beauty (beau, joli), Age (jeune, vieux, nouveau), Goodness (bon, mauvais), Size (grand, petit).

un grand homme · une belle fleur · des livres intéressants (after, because it is not a BAGS adjective).
