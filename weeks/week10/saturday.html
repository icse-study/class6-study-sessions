The Indus Valley Civilisation · Urban Local Self-Government
India's first cities, planned on a grid 4,500 years ago — and how our cities are governed today.
🏛️ History — The Indus Valley Civilisation

When and where

The Indus Valley or Harappan Civilisation flourished from about 2500 BCE to 1500 BCE. It is named after Harappa, the first site to be excavated.

Key sites: Harappa, Mohenjodaro (Sindhi for ‘mound of the dead’), Lothal, Dholavira, Kalibangan, Ropar.

Town planning — the civilisation's greatest achievement

Each city had a raised citadel and a lower town. Streets crossed at right angles in a grid. Houses were of baked brick, with courtyards, bathrooms and private wells.

The drainage system was the finest of the ancient world: every house was connected to a street drain, the drains were covered with stone slabs, and inspection holes were left at intervals for cleaning.

💡 A covered, connected drain in every street shows a strong civic sense — and a city government able to enforce rules.

Economy, seals and religion

The main occupation was agriculture — wheat, barley and cotton (the Harappans were the first people known to grow cotton). They also made pottery, wove cloth and worked metal, and traded with Mesopotamia, where Harappan seals have been found.

Seals were cut from steatite and carry animal motifs and a script of about 400 pictographs that has never been deciphered.

Religion: seals show a figure identified as Pashupati (a proto-Shiva) and a Mother Goddess; they also worshipped trees such as the peepal, and animals.

💡 The horse was unknown to the Harappans — no horse appears on any seal. It arrives later, with the Aryans.

Decline

Nobody knows for certain. The suggested causes are floods, earthquakes, a change in the course of the river, deforestation and the drying of the land, or invasion — possibly several together.

Who sits in a Municipal Corporation

Councillors — elected from wards; one-third of the seats are reserved for women, with seats for SC and ST as well.
Aldermen — experienced people nominated for their expertise.
Mayor — the political executive, who presides over meetings.
Commissioner — the real executive, appointed by the state government, who runs the administration.

💡 Mayor = elected political head. Commissioner = appointed administrative head. Examiners love this distinction.

Functions and money

Obligatory (must do): water supply, roads, street lighting, sanitation, public health — hospitals and vaccination, primary education, fire service, registration of births and deaths.
Optional (if funds allow): public libraries, museums, parks, cultural events.
Income: octroi, toll tax and property tax, plus grants from the state government.

💡 Income tax is not a municipal tax — it goes to the central government.
✍️ Describe the drainage system of the Indus Valley cities and explain why it was remarkable.
Model answer →
Every house in a Harappan city had its own bathroom, and the waste water ran out through a small channel into a larger drain that ran along the street. These street drains were built of brick and covered with stone slabs, with inspection holes left at regular intervals so that they could be cleaned. This was remarkable because no other civilisation of the time — not Egypt, not Mesopotamia — had anything like it. It shows both a very high standard of civic sense and a city authority strong enough to plan the whole system and keep it in repair.
✍️ Explain the difference between a Municipality and a Municipal Corporation.
Model answer →
A Municipality, or Municipal Committee, governs a smaller city of roughly 20,000 to one lakh people and is headed by a Chairperson. A Municipal Corporation governs a metropolitan city of more than ten lakh people, such as Mumbai or Chennai, and is headed by a Mayor as political head with a Commissioner appointed by the state as its real executive. A Corporation has far larger funds, more departments and wider powers, though both are elected bodies performing the same kinds of obligatory duties — water, sanitation, roads, health and primary education.
