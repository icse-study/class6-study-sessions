🧬 Saturday · Week 17 · Diagram Lab
Inside the pump 🫀
The heart is drawn as if you are facing the person — so the heart's right side appears on the left of the page. Learn the heart, then the two-loop map, then feel your own pulse.

🫀 Section of the human heart

Blue = deoxygenated blood (right side). Red = oxygenated blood (left side). The left ventricle has the thickest wall.

🔁 The two circuits

Top loop = pulmonary circulation (heart ⇄ lungs). Bottom loop = systemic circulation (heart ⇄ body).
🔬 Pulse lab

A. At rest

Sit quietly. Place the first three fingers of one hand on the inside of the other wrist, on the thumb side. Count the throbs for 15 seconds and multiply by 4.

B. After exercise

Run up and down the stairs 2–3 times and count again at once. Then rest for 15 minutes and count once more.
👀 The pulse jumps (it may reach 120 or more) and then slowly comes back down.
✅ Working muscles need more oxygen and food, so the heart pumps faster.

🎯 Fun Trivia
• 🩺 The stethoscope was invented in 1816 by the French doctor René Laennec — he rolled up a sheet of paper into a tube to listen to a patient's heart.
• 🐭 A mouse's heart beats about 500 times a minute; an elephant's only about 30.
• 🩸 One donation of blood can help save up to three lives, because it can be split into parts.
