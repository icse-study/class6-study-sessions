🌍 Geography · Ch 7 South America · Part 2
The Andes, the Ring of Fire & Machu Picchu
The longest mountain chain on Earth, active volcanoes of the Ring of Fire, the world's highest navigable lake, and an Inca city built to survive earthquakes.

🗺️ Five physical divisions

1. Andes Mountains 2. Eastern Highlands 3. Central Lowlands 4. Coastal Plains 5. Islands.

🏔️ The Andes

The longest chain of fold mountains in the world, over 7,000 km, along the west coast from the Venezuelan coast to Cape Horn. They cross 7 countries: Venezuela, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, Argentina, Chile. Part of the Western Cordilleras continuing south from North America. Second highest range after the Himalayas. Young fold mountains, narrower than the Rockies.

🧱 How the Andes formed

Rocks on the Earth's surface were compressed and folded by forces in the Earth's interior. This continues today, so the region is prone to earthquakes and volcanoes.

💡 Young fold mountains: formed in the last ~2 million years; parallel ranges; steep slopes; sharp high crests.

⛰️ Peaks

Aconcagua (6,959 m) — highest peak of the Americas, the "Roof of the Americas". Cotopaxi (5,911 m) — one of the highest active volcanoes in the world. High summits are mostly snow-covered.

🔥 Pacific Ring of Fire

A zone of earthquakes and volcanic activity surrounding the Pacific Ocean. Many Andean peaks are volcanic because the Andes are part of it.

🏞️ Bolivian Plateau & Titicaca

The Andes have two or three parallel ranges with intermontane plateaus. The Bolivian Plateau is the most extensive high plateau in the world after the Tibetan Plateau. Lake Titicaca, located here, is the highest navigable water body in the world (traditional reed boats).

🏛️ Machu Picchu

A 15th-century Inca marvel about 80 km north-west of Cusco, Peru. Its stone blocks are so well cut and fitted that they "dance" or "bounce" — they adjust during earthquakes and do not topple. Almost 60% of construction is underground. UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1983.

🎯 Must-remember facts
• Andes > 7,000 km · 7 countries · 2nd highest after Himalayas · young fold · narrower than Rockies
• Aconcagua 6,959 m = Roof of the Americas · Cotopaxi 5,911 m active volcano
• Ring of Fire around the Pacific
• Bolivian Plateau 2nd only to Tibet · Titicaca highest navigable
• Machu Picchu: Inca, 15th c., 80 km NW of Cusco, UNESCO 1983
