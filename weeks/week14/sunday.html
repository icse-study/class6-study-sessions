🔬 Science & Space

🐆 A new wild cat species — 17 Sep

A genetic study identified Leopardus tilcayo — the first entirely new living wild cat species found in more than 100 years. Leopardus is the group of small wild cats of Central and South America, like the ocelot.

📖 Reading is good for your brain — 15 Sep

A review of many studies found that reading is linked to better memory and thinking, and a lower risk of dementia in old age.

🛠️ Engineers' Day — 15 Sep

India's Engineers' Day is the birthday of Sir M. Visvesvaraya from Karnataka, who designed the Krishna Raja Sagara (KRS) dam near Mysuru and received the Bharat Ratna (1955).

🌍 World Ozone Day — 16 Sep

Marks the Montreal Protocol (1987), the agreement that phased out CFCs — chemicals that damage the ozone layer, which shields us from the Sun's ultraviolet (UV) rays.

🎨 Arts & Culture

🐘 Ganesh Chaturthi & Hindi Diwas — 14 Sep

Ganesh Chaturthi, the festival of Lord Ganesha, fell on 14 September. The same day was Hindi Diwas, marking 14 September 1949, when the Constituent Assembly adopted Hindi in Devanagari script as an official language of India.

📺 Emmy Awards — 14 Sep

The 78th Primetime Emmy Awards, the top prizes for television, were held in Los Angeles. The Pitt won Best Drama Series.

💡 Remember: Emmys = TV · Oscars = films · Grammys = music.

🎬 Toronto Film Festival — 20 Sep

At TIFF in Canada, the audience vote — the People's Choice Award — went to the Spanish film La Bola Negra.

🏅 Sports

🏟️ Asian Games open in Japan — 19 Sep

The 20th Asian Games began in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan (19 Sep – 4 Oct), opened by Emperor Naruhito. India sent about 500 athletes. The flag-bearers were shooter Manu Bhaker and kabaddi star Pawan Sehrawat.

🥈 India's first medals — 20 Sep

India's first medal was a silver in the women's 10 m air rifle team event — Elavenil Valarivan, Sonam Maskar and Vidarsa Vinod scored 1898.0, second to China. Elavenil also won silver in the individual final.

♟️ Chess Olympiad begins — 15 Sep

The 46th Chess Olympiad started in Samarkand, Uzbekistan (15–27 Sep) with a record number of teams. India is the defending champion in both the Open and Women's sections, having won both golds at Budapest 2024. Gukesh D won his first five games.

🇫🇷 Sunday · Week 14 · Révision
La négation — révision
Workbook pages 29–32 and textbook pages 58–59. The mistakes students make most often with ne … pas, and how to fix them.
⚠️ Common mistakes — and the fix

Keep the original verb

✗ Elles ne parlons pas italiennes.
✓ Elles ne sont pas italiennes.

💡 Negation never changes the verb — it only wraps it.

pas goes right after the verb, not at the end

✗ Elle n'a quinze ans pas.
✓ Elle n'a pas quinze ans.
✗ Les enfants ne sont dans la maison pas.
✓ Les enfants ne sont pas dans la maison.

Change the person in your answer

Tu es journaliste ? → Non, je ne suis pas journaliste.
Vous avez le livre de français ? → Non, nous n'avons pas le livre de français.
Tu vas au concert ? → Non, je ne vais pas au concert.

💡 Nous goes with avons, never avez.

-ir verbs keep their -iss-

Daniel et Sophie réussissent ? → Non, ils ne réussissent pas aux examens.
Sophie et Julie ne choisissent pas la robe noire.

🖼️ Posez la question (workbook p.32)

Picture → question → answer

🚗🚗 → Qu'est-ce que c'est ? — Ce sont des voitures.
👩👩👩 → Qui est-ce ? — Ce sont des femmes.
🌳 → Qu'est-ce que c'est ? — C'est un arbre.
👧 → Qui est-ce ? — C'est une fille.

🎯 Fun Trivia
French has other negative pairs that work exactly like ne … pas: ne … jamais (never) and ne … plus (no longer). Je ne mange jamais de fromage — I never eat cheese.
