🗣️ ಕನ್ನಡ — quick recall

ಮಕ್ಕಳ ವಚನ + ಸಂಧಿ

The vachanas are short, poetic sayings teaching ಸತ್ಯ (truth), ಪ್ರಾಮಾಣಿಕತೆ (honesty), ಶಿಸ್ತು (discipline) and respect for ಹಿರಿಯರು (elders).

ಸಂಧಿ merges two sounds where words meet, to ease pronunciation. ಸ್ವರ ಸಂಧಿ joins vowels: ದೇವ + ಆಲಯ = ದೇವಾಲಯ · ವಿದ್ಯಾ + ಅರ್ಥಿ = ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿ · ಸೂರ್ಯ + ಉದಯ = ಸೂರ್ಯೋದಯ.
🇫🇷 French — quick recall

Adjective agreement and position

Add ‑e for feminine, ‑s for plural. Most adjectives follow the noun; the BAGS group (Beauty, Age, Goodness, Size) comes before it.

grand → grande → grands → grandes · bon → bonne → bonnes · beau → belle · vieux → vieille · nouveau → nouveaux.
🌍 Geography + 🏛️ History — combined

Both halves in one place

Rotation (24 hours, on its axis) gives day and night. Revolution (365¼ days, around the Sun) combined with the 23.5° tilt gives the seasons. Solstices: 21 June and 22 December. Equinoxes: 21 March and 23 September.

History: Chandragupta Maurya founded the empire in the 4th century BCE, advised by Chanakya. Ashoka embraced Buddhism after the Kalinga War and spread Dhamma through rock and pillar edicts. Civics: the Mayor heads a Municipal Corporation, elected by ward Councillors.

📚 Key Definitions

Learn these word for word

Factor — a number that divides another exactly, with no remainder.

Rational number — any number expressible as p/q where q ≠ 0.
Reflection — the bouncing back of light from a surface.

📌 Things to Remember
⚠️ Don't confuse these

Maths — HCF vs LCM. HCF is the largest common factor (a small answer); LCM is the smallest common multiple (a large answer). If your HCF is bigger than both numbers, something has gone wrong.

Maths — median. Always sort the data first. With an even count, average the two middle values.

Maths — triangles. Never forget the ½ in the area formula.

Geography. Rotation causes day and night; revolution plus the 23.5° tilt causes the seasons. Distance from the Sun does not.

Physics. All angles in reflection are measured from the normal, not from the surface.

Biology. The liver produces bile; the gall bladder only stores it.

History. Ashoka embraced Buddhism after the Kalinga War, not before it.

English. A modal is always followed by the base verb: "can swim", never "can swims".

French. BAGS adjectives come before the noun; everything else comes after it.
